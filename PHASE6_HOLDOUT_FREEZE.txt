===============================================================================
PHASE 6 - THE 2026-27 HOLDOUT FREEZE
WRITTEN BEFORE ANY 2026-27 MATCH IS SCORED
===============================================================================

STATUS      Declared 2026-09-03. AWAITING SIGN-OFF. Nothing in this file has
            been run against a 2026-27 result and no 2026-27 result has been
            looked at.

WHY IT EXISTS, AND WHY IT IS URGENT

    Every figure this project has produced is a WALK-FORWARD DEVELOPMENT
    ESTIMATE. 2025-26 was scored during Phase 3's lambda sweep and its B0-B6
    ablation, so every number is optimistic by an unknown margin. The market
    benchmark it is measured against is not.

    A genuine holdout was supposed to come from a pre-season freeze. The
    2026-27 season started 21 August 2026, a week later than usual because of
    the World Cup, and that freeze was missed. This is the replacement:
    freeze now, declare a cutoff date, score forward from it.

    Matchweeks 1 and 2 are played, about 20 matches. Those matches WARM THE
    RATING STATE and are NOT SCORED. About 360 remain, within 5% of a full
    season.

    EVERY WEEK OF DELAY COSTS ABOUT TEN MATCHES OFF THE HOLDOUT. That is why
    this file is written before the twenty-match persistence question, before
    any xG decision, and before anything else.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  THE MODEL.  EXACTLY ONE.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

H1.1  DIXON-COLES WALK-FORWARD IS THE FROZEN MODEL.

          scripts/phase2_poisson_dixon_coles.py
          variant  dc_walkforward   (PRIMARY_VARIANT)

H1.2  WHY, AND THE ARGUMENT IS NOT "IT HAS THE BEST NUMBER".

      E1a's SoT ratings have a LOWER point estimate - 0.98124 against DC's
      0.99036 - and it would be easy to freeze E1a on that. It is the wrong
      choice, for the reason the brief gives and one more.

      E1a - Dixon-Coles = -0.00912, 95% CI [-0.02177, +0.00391], NOT
      SIGNIFICANT. Freezing E1a would be SELECTING ON AN UNRESOLVED
      DIFFERENCE - picking the arm that happened to land lower on the very
      1,520 matches whose optimism this holdout exists to measure. That is
      the contaminated move in its purest form: the holdout would be
      measuring a choice the holdout was supposed to test.

      THE SECOND REASON IS DEPENDENCE. E1a needs shot-on-target counts for
      every match, from football-data.co.uk, in-season, on a weekly cadence.
      Dixon-Coles needs the scoreline. A holdout that can fail because a
      shot column arrives late or changes definition mid-season is a worse
      instrument than one that cannot, and E2.2 already recorded a season
      (2023-24) sitting high on every shot column at once.

      THE THIRD IS THAT DC IS THE PROJECT'S OWN REFERENCE. Every ladder rung
      from D0 to D4, every Phase 5 rung, and the tier-2 recency
      decomposition are all reported against it. A holdout on DC prices the
      optimism of the whole reported set, not of one arm.

H1.3  WHAT IS NOT FROZEN, STATED SO IT CANNOT BE ADDED LATER. No ladder
      rung, no penalised logistic, no ensemble, no blend of DC with Elo or
      with the market. ONE MODEL. If a second is wanted, it needed declaring
      here and it was not.

H1.4  D0 AND THE MARKET ARE SCORED ALONGSIDE AS BENCHMARKS, not as
      candidates. Section 6. Odds never enter a design matrix or a rating
      window; that prohibition is unchanged and unconditional.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.  EVERYTHING THAT MAKES IT REPRODUCIBLE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

      Anything a future session could tune is a NUMBER here. If a value is
      not in this section it may not be chosen later; it must be read from
      the named source file at the named symbol.

H2.1  THE RATING AND ITS CONSTANTS, all from
      scripts/phase2_poisson_dixon_coles.py, "THE LOCKED SPECIFICATION":

          MAX_GOALS                     25
          TIME_DECAY_HALF_LIFE_DAYS     107.0
          RHO_LOWER                     -0.5
          RHO_UPPER                     +0.5
          RHO_GRID_POINTS               60
          RHO_GOLDEN_ITERATIONS         60
          SCALING_MAX_ITERATIONS        200
          SCALING_TOLERANCE             1e-10
          NEUTRAL_STRENGTH              1.0
          MIN_RATE                      1e-6

      The half-life is ln(2)/0.0065 = 106.6 days from Dixon & Coles (1997),
      rounded to 107.0. IT WAS TAKEN FROM THE LITERATURE AND HAS NEVER BEEN
      FITTED IN THIS PROJECT. It is not fitted now and may not be.

H2.2  THE ESTIMATOR. Multiplicative iterative scaling - the closed-form
      coordinate solution of the weighted Poisson likelihood. Deterministic,
      no solver dependency, no random seed.

      INITIALISATION: attack = 1.0 for every team, defence = 1.0 for every
      team, home_multiplier = 1.0. Not a tuned start; the objective is
      strictly convex in the transformed parameters and the fixed point is
      unique.

      SCALE PINNING: attack has a free multiplicative scale (A -> cA,
      D -> D/c leaves both rates unchanged). It is pinned by setting the
      GEOMETRIC MEAN of attack to 1 at the end of every iteration.

      CONVERGENCE: max absolute change across the concatenated
      (attack, defence, home_multiplier) vector below SCALING_TOLERANCE, or
      SCALING_MAX_ITERATIONS reached.

      DEGENERATE BOUNDARY MLE: a team that scored no goals in the window
      gets attack exactly 0, one that conceded none gets defence exactly 0;
      both are replaced by NEUTRAL_STRENGTH. Unchanged.

H2.3  THE TIME DECAY. weight = 0.5 ** (age_in_days / 107.0), where age is
      measured from the CUTOFF, not from the season start.

H2.4  REFIT CADENCE: ONE REFIT PER DISTINCT MATCH DATE. For each date on
      which at least one scored fixture is played, the model is refit on the
      window and every fixture on that date is predicted from that one fit.

H2.5  THE WINDOW RULE: STRICT. date < cutoff. A match played on the same day
      as the match being predicted is NEVER in the window. This is the rule
      the frozen folds use and it is not relaxed for a live season.

H2.6  THE WINDOW'S SCOPE, AND THIS IS THE ONE GENUINELY NEW DECISION.

      The frozen folds scoped each window to (train_seasons + test_season).
      For the holdout the equivalent is ALL FIVE DATASET SEASONS PLUS
      2026-27 TO DATE:

          2021-22, 2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25, 2025-26, and every 2026-27
          match with date strictly before the cutoff

      DECLARED NOW because it is the only structural choice the holdout
      forces, and it must not be picked later. Note that the 107-day
      half-life makes seasons before 2025-26 nearly weightless in practice -
      a match two years old carries 0.5 ** (730/107) = 0.9% of a match on
      the cutoff - so this choice is declared for reproducibility rather
      than because it is expected to matter.

H2.7  RHO. Fitted per window by deterministic bracketed golden section on
      the profile likelihood, on ACTUAL GOALS, over matches where both sides
      scored at most one. Bracket and iteration counts as H2.1. Where no
      such match exists in the window, rho = 0.0.

H2.8  FROM RATES TO PROBABILITIES. Score matrix to MAX_GOALS = 25 with the
      Dixon-Coles tau correction on the four cells 0-0, 1-0, 0-1, 1-1;
      P(H) = lower triangle, P(D) = trace, P(A) = upper triangle, each
      divided by the matrix mass. outcome_probabilities(), unchanged.

      NO TRANSFORMATION IS APPLIED AFTERWARDS. No shrinkage toward the base
      rate, no calibration, no temperature, no clipping beyond what the
      score matrix produces. A calibrated holdout is not a holdout.

H2.9  THE METRICS - the full Phase 0 set, in this order:

          accuracy, balanced_accuracy, macro_f1, log_loss, brier_score, rps

      LOG LOSS AND RPS ARE PRIMARY. RPS must agree in sign with log loss on
      any comparison or that comparison is INCONCLUSIVE.

H2.10 THE MARKET BENCHMARK. Bet365 closing prices, PROPORTIONAL de-vig:

          p_i = (1/o_i) / sum_j (1/o_j)

      That is B4.1 of PHASE5_MARKET_PREDECLARATION.txt, the declared
      primary, unchanged. Shin remains the declared sensitivity and is
      reported alongside. No blending of bookmakers, no per-season
      recalibration.

H2.11 THE PAIRED BOOTSTRAP. 10,000 draws, seed 20260901, per-match paired,
      95% percentile intervals. Every delta reported carries one.

H2.12 TEAM NAMES ARE PART OF THE SPECIFICATION, AND THIS IS NOT
      PEDANTRY. The dataset's vocabulary is its own: "Manchester Utd", not
      "Man United"; "Nottingham", not "Nott'm Forest"; "Ipswich Town", not
      "Ipswich". A name that fails to match does not raise - it makes a
      known team look like a team with no history and silently hands it
      NEUTRAL_STRENGTH (section 4). The scoring run MUST assert that every
      2026-27 team name either matches a dataset name exactly or is one of
      the three declared promoted sides, and FAIL otherwise.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.  THE CUTOFF DATE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

H3.1  THE RULE: the cutoff is THE FIRST 2026-27 FIXTURE DATE ON OR AFTER THE
      DATE THIS FILE IS COMMITTED. Every 2026-27 match with a date strictly
      earlier than the cutoff WARMS THE STATE and is NOT SCORED. Every match
      on or after the cutoff is scored.

H3.2  THE DATE IS PINNED AT SIGN-OFF, NOT BY THIS FILE, and the reason is
      stated rather than glossed: THE 2026-27 FIXTURE LIST IS NOT IN THIS
      REPOSITORY. data/raw holds 2021-22 to 2025-26 only. Writing a date
      here from memory would be exactly the class of unverifiable claim this
      project has already been bitten by three times.

      So the rule above is the declaration, and the SIGN-OFF COMMIT PINS THE
      DATE by recording it in this file's amendment block below, from the
      published fixture list, before any 2026-27 result is scored.

      EXPECTED, from the calendar as described: matchweeks 1 and 2 are
      complete as of 2026-09-03 and an international break follows, so the
      cutoff is expected to be the first matchweek-3 date, around 12
      September 2026. IF THE ACTUAL FIXTURE LIST DISAGREES, THE RULE WINS,
      NOT THIS EXPECTATION.

H3.3  THE EXPECTED SCORED-MATCH COUNT.

          full season                        380
          matchweeks 1-2, warming only       -20
          -------------------------------------
          EXPECTED SCORED                    360

      360 / 380 = 94.7% of a season, within the 5% the brief allows.

      THE COUNT IS ASSERTED, NOT ASSUMED. The scoring run must report the
      actual number of scored matches and state the difference from 360.
      Postponements, and any matchweek-2 fixture moved beyond the cutoff,
      change it, and a changed count is reported rather than absorbed.

H3.4  NO MATCH IS EVER MOVED ACROSS THE CUTOFF. A fixture postponed from
      before the cutoff to after it is scored, because the cutoff is a DATE
      and the rule is about dates played, not about matchweek labels. Stated
      now so it cannot be decided later in whichever direction helps.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.  THE PROMOTED-TEAM EXPOSURE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

H4.1  WHAT THE DATASET ACTUALLY CONTAINS, checked rather than assumed:

          Coventry        NOT IN THE DATASET under any spelling tried
          Hull            NOT IN THE DATASET under any spelling tried
          Ipswich Town    PRESENT, 2024-25 ONLY - one season, 38 matches

      So TWO of the three promoted sides have ZERO Premier League history in
      the five-season window, and the third has one season which the 107-day
      half-life renders nearly weightless by September 2026.

H4.2  THE EXPOSURE, as arithmetic rather than an impression:

          matches involving Coventry or Hull, full season
              38 + 38 - 2 (they meet twice)              =  74
          matches involving any of the three promoted
              38 x 3 - 6 (three pairs, twice each)       = 108

          74 / 380  = 19.5% of a season involves a ZERO-HISTORY side
          108 / 380 = 28.4% involves a promoted side

      Scaled to the 360 scored, roughly 70 and 102 respectively. THESE ARE
      ESTIMATES FROM THE FIXTURE ARITHMETIC. The scoring run reports the
      actual counts and a metric breakdown split by them.

H4.3  THE INITIALISATION APPLIES WITHOUT AMENDMENT, and here is exactly what
      it does. A team absent from the fitting window is absent from the
      estimator's team list entirely. At prediction, match_rates() maps the
      team name to the fitted attack and defence and calls
      .fillna(NEUTRAL_STRENGTH):

          attack  = 1.0
          defence = 1.0

      A zero-history side is therefore predicted as an EXACTLY AVERAGE team,
      with the home multiplier and rho still doing their work. Elo v1's
      equivalent - INITIAL_RATING = 1500.0 flat, K_FACTOR = 20.0,
      HOME_ADVANTAGE = 60.0 - is recorded here for completeness; Elo is not
      the frozen model and is not scored.

H4.4  THIS IS DECLARED NOW AND MAY NOT BE ADJUSTED MID-SEASON. If Coventry
      and Hull are, say, relegation-bound and the flat prior costs the model
      badly through September, THAT IS A RESULT AND NOT A DEFECT. Every
      development figure this holdout is measured against was produced under
      the same rule, so changing it would break the comparison the holdout
      exists to make.

H4.5  WHAT THIS EXPOSURE MEANS FOR THE READ, STATED IN ADVANCE. 19.5% of
      matches carrying a flat-prior side is HIGHER than the development
      folds typically saw, so a holdout figure worse than 0.99036 is
      partially explained by promotion churn rather than by development
      optimism, and the two are NOT SEPARABLE by this design. The split in
      H4.2 is reported precisely so the size of that confound is visible
      rather than argued about afterwards.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.  THE PROHIBITION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

H5.1  NO FEATURE, HYPERPARAMETER, RATING, INITIALISATION, WINDOW RULE,
      TRANSFORMATION OR EVALUATION DECISION MAY BE CHANGED BECAUSE OF
      ANYTHING OBSERVED IN 2026-27.

      Not the half-life. Not MAX_GOALS. Not the rho bracket. Not the
      promoted-team prior. Not the metric set. Not the de-vig. Not the model
      choice in section 1.

H5.2  THE PROHIBITION COVERS LOOKING, NOT ONLY CHANGING. Scoring is run
      ONCE, at season end (section 6). Interim scoring is not forbidden
      because it is dangerous in itself but because a number seen in
      November cannot be unseen in December, and every decision after it is
      taken by someone who has seen it.

H5.3  IF A DEFECT IS FOUND THAT FORCES A CHANGE, it is disclosed as a dated
      amendment in this file, stating what was changed, why, and what had
      already been observed at the time. AND THE HOLDOUT IS REPORTED AS
      COMPROMISED - explicitly, in REPORTS.md, in the same entry as any
      figure it produces.

      A compromised holdout is still worth reporting. A compromised holdout
      reported as a clean one is worth less than nothing, because it would
      retire the question while getting it wrong.

H5.4  THE ONE THING THAT IS NOT A CHANGE: fixing a defect that would prevent
      the declared model from running at all - a crash, a join that drops
      rows, a name that fails to match under H2.12. Repairing the
      implementation so that it does what this file already says is not
      amending what this file says. Any such repair is still logged with its
      date, and the distinction is argued in the log rather than assumed.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.  THE SCORING PROCEDURE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

H6.1  RUN ONCE, AT SEASON END, after the final 2026-27 fixture is played.

H6.2  WHAT IS SCORED, on the scored matches only:

      (a) Dixon-Coles walk-forward, the frozen model, full Phase 0 metric set
      (b) D0, the base rate, computed on the SAME rule Phase 2 used
      (c) the market, Bet365 closing, proportional de-vig, on the same
          matches, with Shin reported as the declared sensitivity

H6.3  THE DELTAS, paired bootstrap, 10,000 draws, seed 20260901:

          DC - market       the gap this project has been competing with
          DC - D0           that the model beats the base rate out of sample
          market - D0       how hard this particular season was to predict

H6.4  AGAINST THE DEVELOPMENT ESTIMATES. The 1,520-match figures this
      holdout is pricing, recorded here so they cannot be misremembered:

          model             acc     bal_acc  macro_f1  log_loss  brier    rps
          ---------------------------------------------------------------------
          Dixon-Coles      0.5263   0.4511   0.3917    0.99036  0.58969  0.20350
          market           0.5513   0.4724   0.4095    0.96057  0.57019  0.19469
          D0 base rate     0.4447   0.3333   0.2052    1.06889  0.64667  0.23185

          DC - market, development                    +0.02979

H6.5  THE QUESTION THE HOLDOUT ANSWERS: did the development estimates hold?
      Reported as the difference between the holdout figure and the
      development figure for each of DC, market and D0, WITH THE MARKET AS
      THE CONTROL. If DC degrades and the market degrades by a similar
      amount, the season was harder and the development estimate largely
      held. If DC degrades and the market does not, the development estimate
      was optimistic and the size of the optimism is the difference.

      THAT CONTROL IS THE POINT OF SCORING THE MARKET ON THE SAME MATCHES,
      and it is why the market is scored even though its verdict is already
      known from Phase 5B.

H6.6  ALSO REPORTED, because H4.5 says the confound must be visible: every
      metric split by whether the match involved a zero-history side.

H6.7  WHAT THE HOLDOUT STILL CANNOT ESTABLISH. One season, ~360 matches,
      resolution on a log-loss delta of roughly 0.01 - WORSE than the 0.005
      the 1,520 development matches gave. It cannot resolve a small
      optimism. It can resolve a large one, and it is the only clean
      measurement this project will ever have of that.

      It also cannot generalise beyond 2026-27. A single season carries its
      own promotion churn, its own injury luck and its own scoring
      environment, and a holdout on one season prices the development
      estimate against THAT season, not against football.

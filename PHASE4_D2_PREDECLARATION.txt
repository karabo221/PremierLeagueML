9.  THE OPEN DECISION - WHY THIS DOCUMENT IS BLOCKED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The DC-passthrough diagnostic ran and returned INVALID on a regime gate,
    not on the link. The finding:

        Phase 2's walk-forward only ever fits windows of at least one full
        training season, and only ever PREDICTS test-season matches. Applying
        the same rule to TRAINING rows enters a regime Phase 2 never did.

        lambda_home reaches 47.26 on a 19-match window (West Ham, 2021-08-23)
        and 21.94 on a 20-match window, against a maximum of 4.64 across all
        1,520 test rows. Dixon-Coles strengths are not identified on a
        fortnight of football.

        Those outliers sit in the TRAINING rows, so they distort the
        standardiser the test rows are then mapped through. That is what
        produced fold 2's passthrough log loss of 27.09 - not the logistic
        link. Reading that number as a link measurement would have been a
        false finding that reshaped the whole Phase 4 design.

        Exactly 380 training rows - the whole of 2021-22 - are fitted on
        windows smaller than the smallest window any test row was scored on.

    THE DECISION REQUIRED, and it must be made before any fit:

        A. BURN-IN BY SEASON. D2's training rows begin at 2022-23; 2021-22
           supplies state but is never a training row. Costs fold 1 its entire
           training set, so fold 1 would have to be dropped or redefined -
           which changes the frozen fold structure and is therefore a serious
           step.

        B. MINIMUM-WINDOW THRESHOLD. Training rows require a fitting window of
           at least N matches. N must be declared before fitting, not chosen
           from the failure. A principled, untuned choice is N = 380, one full
           season, which is also the smallest window any test row has - it
           makes training and test regimes identical by construction.

        C. WINSORISE / CLIP the state features at bounds fitted on training
           rows only. Keeps every row but changes the feature definition, and
           the bound is a new free parameter.

        D. Something else.

    RECOMMENDATION: B with N = 380. It is the only option whose threshold is
    fixed by the experiment's own structure rather than by the observed
    failure - it equalises the training and test regimes by definition - and
    it keeps all four frozen folds intact, which A does not.

    NOT CHOSEN HERE. Choosing a burn-in threshold having already seen which
    value broke fold 2 is precisely what this project's pre-declarations exist
    to prevent. The decision is recorded as an amendment before anything is
    fitted.

AMENDMENT 2 - BURN-IN THRESHOLD, AND AN EXTENDED LAMBDA GRID
Written before the re-run. Section 9 is unblocked by this.
===============================================================================

A2.1  THE BURN-IN THRESHOLD IS 380, AND IT IS DERIVED, NOT TUNED

    G7 requires every training row to come from a fitting window at least as
    large as the smallest window any test row was scored on.

    That smallest window is 380 BY CONSTRUCTION, and the derivation needs no
    data at all:

        test rows are only ever scored inside a test season;
        every test season is preceded by at least one complete season;
        a complete season is 380 matches;
        therefore no test row is ever scored on a window below 380.

    From which the threshold follows as a fixed point, not a choice:

        below 380  admits training rows fitted on evidence no test row
                   ever sees - the model would be taught in a regime it is
                   never used in
        above 380  discards training rows whose regime the test set
                   genuinely contains - throwing away valid evidence

    380 is the only value that makes the training and test regimes identical.
    It was derivable before anything ran, and this amendment asserts it from
    the structure above rather than from any observed number.

    ON 47.26, AND WHY IT IS NOT AN INPUT HERE

    lambda_home reaching 47.26 on a 19-match window is the observation that
    prompted G7 to EXIST. It is cited for that, and for nothing else. It is
    not an input to the threshold's value, and the threshold would be 380 had
    the largest early lambda been 5 or 500.

    That distinction is the entire difference between this amendment and the
    pattern Phase 3's Amendment 2 fell into, where a rule was changed after
    the result was known and the outcome moved with it. Here the failure
    prompted a gate; the gate's threshold comes from the fold structure.

A2.2  THE COST, STATED PLAINLY

    Under the gate, 2021-22 contributes NO training rows - all 380 of its
    matches are fitted on windows below 380, most of them far below.

    Fold 1 trains on 2021-22 alone. So fold 1 has no training set under the
    gate and the DC-PASSTHROUGH DIAGNOSTIC RUNS ON FOLDS 2, 3 AND 4 ONLY.

    That is acceptable for this diagnostic and only this one: it answers a
    yes/no question about whether the logistic link preserves Dixon-Coles, and
    three folds and 1,140 test matches answer it.

    IT DOES NOT PROPAGATE TO D2. All four frozen outer folds remain in force
    for the ladder. If the burn-in gate turns out to bind on D2's own training
    rows, that is a separate question requiring its own declaration - it is
    NOT inherited from this amendment.

    CONSEQUENCE FOR THE COMPARISON FIGURE: dc_walkforward's 0.9904 is a
    four-fold number. A three-fold passthrough may not be compared against it.
    DC's figure is recomputed on folds 2-4 only, and every delta and interval
    in the re-run is against that recomputed figure.

A2.3  THE LAMBDA GRID IS EXTENDED DOWNWARD - DECLARED BEFORE THE RE-RUN

    Two of four folds selected lambda at the grid floor of 0.01. A floor
    selection means the grid was wrong for this feature regime, and Phase 4
    section 8 requires it be reported rather than absorbed.

    This is independent of the window problem and is fixed independently.

    NEW GRID, 21 points, half-decade spacing throughout, extending the
    existing 13 downward by four decades:

        0.000001, 0.000003, 0.00001, 0.00003, 0.0001, 0.0003,
        0.001,    0.003,
        0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, 10, 30, 100, 300, 1000, 3000, 10000

    The upper 13 are unchanged, so any earlier selection remains
    representable and the two grids agree wherever they overlap.

    A PREDICTION, RECORDED NOW SO IT CANNOT BE RETROFITTED

    The passthrough design carries exactly TWO features against 760 or more
    training rows. That is a regime in which the unpenalised maximum-likelihood
    fit is well posed and essentially optimal, so the inner CV has little
    reason to prefer any positive penalty. The floor may therefore continue to
    bind however far the grid is extended, and if it does that is NOT the
    pathology G6 was written to catch - it is the correct answer for p = 2.

    G6 is nevertheless left as a hard FAIL rather than softened. Per the
    brief, a floor selection after the G7 gate is a STOP-and-report, and the
    reading above is offered as an interpretation for that report, not as a
    licence to continue.

A2.4  SECTION 9 IS UNBLOCKED

    Option B is adopted at N = 380. Sections 1-8 are unchanged. Nothing else
    in this document moves.

AMENDMENT 3 - G6 IS SCOPED TO THE REGIME WHERE IT IS WELL POSED
Written before any re-evaluation. Applicability fixed for every planned rung.
===============================================================================

A3.0  DISCLOSURE, FIRST

    This amendment is written HAVING SEEN folds 3 and 4 of the DC-passthrough
    select lambda at the extended grid's floor of 1e-6. That is stated plainly
    here, exactly as Amendment 2 stated its relationship to 47.26, because an
    amendment written after a failure is the pattern this project polices
    hardest.

    The test that makes it legitimate is the same one: the applicability
    condition below must be derivable from the REGIME - from p and n and the
    conditioning of the fit - and must not reference which rungs failed. If it
    happens to exclude the passthrough, that must fall out of its two features
    against hundreds of rows, and would have fallen out identically had every
    fold selected lambda = 1000.

    It does. The condition is an external, pre-existing statistical criterion,
    imported rather than fitted, and its value for every planned rung is
    tabulated in A3.2 before any of them run.

A3.1  WHAT G6 IS ACTUALLY FOR, AND WHEN THAT QUESTION HAS AN ANSWER

    G6 exists to catch a MIS-SPECIFIED GRID: the data wants a smaller penalty
    than the grid offers, so the selected lambda is an artefact of where the
    grid was cut rather than a property of the data.

    That failure mode presupposes the penalty is doing work. If the
    unpenalised maximum-likelihood fit is itself well identified, then
    lambda -> 0 is not an artefact, it is the correct answer, and no extension
    of the grid will ever stop the floor binding because there is no interior
    optimum to find. In that regime G6 asks a question with no answer.

    So G6 is not failing. It is MALFORMED outside the regime it was written
    for, and this amendment gives it that regime explicitly.

    THE CONDITION: EVENTS PER VARIABLE

        EPV = (count of the RAREST outcome class in the rung's training rows)
              / (design-matrix width, excluding the intercept)

        G6 IS APPLICABLE   when EPV <  10
        G6 IS NOT APPLICABLE when EPV >= 10

    The threshold of 10 is the long-standing events-per-variable rule for
    logistic regression (Peduzzi et al., 1996, and the replication literature
    that followed it). It is the conventional dividing line below which an
    unpenalised logistic fit is unreliable - biased coefficients, unstable
    standard errors, separation risk - and above which it is considered well
    identified.

    THIS THRESHOLD IS IMPORTED, NOT FITTED. It predates this project, it was
    not chosen by looking at any number produced here, and it is not tuned to
    put the passthrough on either side of the line. The rarest class is used
    rather than n because the multinomial fit's precision is governed by the
    scarcest outcome - draws - not by the total.

A4.0  DISCLOSURE, FIRST

    THIS AMENDMENT IS WRITTEN HAVING SEEN THE CONTAMINATION TABLE that
    section 3 of the D0/D1/D2 report produced. That is stated plainly here,
    exactly as Amendments 2 and 3 stated their relationship to the numbers
    that prompted them, because an amendment written after a result is the

    The table:

      fold  column                 SD used   SD (rows >=380)   SD test  inflation
      -----------------------------------------------------------------------
      2     rel_attack_diff          1.059            0.585      0.499     1.81x
      2     rel_defence_diff         3.630            0.375      0.451     9.69x
      2     expected_total_goals    21.161            0.642      0.594    32.97x
      3     rel_defence_diff         2.976            0.414      0.430     7.18x
      3     expected_total_goals    17.280            0.633      0.544    27.32x
      4     rel_defence_diff         2.587            0.420      0.359     6.17x
      4     expected_total_goals    14.967            0.605      0.483    24.73x

    expected_total_goals is standardised by an SD 25-33 times larger than the
    regime it operates in. The whole test season spans 0.14 to 0.25 of one
    standardised unit. rel_defence_diff is 6-10x inflated. Those are exactly
    the two columns whose fitted coefficients returned zero at every fold.

    THE TEST THAT MAKES THIS LEGITIMATE, and it is a different test from the
    one Amendments 2 and 3 had to pass:

        This amendment does not change what is estimated. It changes the
        ESTIMATOR of a quantity the pre-declaration already named.

        Section 6 says continuous features are standardised to unit variance
        on training statistics. It does not say by which estimator of the
        scale. The sample standard deviation was the implicit choice and it
        has a known, textbook failure mode - unbounded sensitivity to
        outliers - which the table above shows firing at 33x.

        The target quantity is unchanged: the training-set scale of the
        column. The amendment replaces a broken estimator of it with a
        robust one, calibrated so that on outlier-free Gaussian data the two
        agree in expectation (A4.2). A column with no outliers is scaled
        identically before and after.

    WHAT WOULD MAKE IT ILLEGITIMATE, stated so it can be checked:

        Selecting the columns by which ones scored badly. The inclusion rule
        in A4.1 is a SOURCE criterion - determinable before any fit, from how
        the column is produced - and it is applied to every column meeting
        it whether or not that column's coefficient was zero.

A4.1  WHICH COLUMNS, AND ON WHAT CRITERION

    ROBUST SCALING APPLIES TO EXACTLY THE COLUMNS DERIVED FROM A DIXON-COLES
    FIT ON A POINT-IN-TIME WINDOW:

        rel_attack_diff
        rel_defence_diff
        expected_total_goals

    EVERY OTHER COLUMN KEEPS SECTION 6 UNCHANGED - all 88 of D1's design
    columns, and rel_elo_diff.

    THE CRITERION IS THE SOURCE, NOT THE SYMPTOM. A column qualifies if its
    value is read out of a Dixon-Coles fit whose window can be small enough
    that the strengths are not identified. That is a property of how the
    column is produced, fixed before anything is fitted, and it is why the
    defect exists: Amendment 2 A2.1 established that DC strengths are not
    identified below a full season, and lambda_home reaches 47.26 on a
    19-match window against 4.64 across all 1,520 test rows.

    rel_elo_diff DOES NOT QUALIFY, on that criterion. It is not read out of a
    DC fit at all; Elo is a sequential update with no fitting window, so it
    has no small-window regime to be undefined in.

    That its measured inflation is 0.88-0.93x - that is, none - is a
    SECOND, INDEPENDENT observation pointing the same way. It is recorded
    because it corroborates the source criterion, and it is NOT the reason
    for the exclusion. Had rel_elo_diff shown 30x inflation it would still
    be excluded here, and would need its own amendment naming its own cause.

    THE SAME THREE COLUMNS TAKE ROBUST SCALING IN EVERY RUNG THAT CARRIES
    THEM, D2 and D2-static alike, so that a comparison between two rungs is
    never a comparison between two scaling rules.

A4.2  THE STATISTIC, DEFINED EXACTLY

    For a qualifying column, on the training rows of the fit in question -
    the outer training rows for an outer fit, and inside the inner CV that
    inner fold's training rows only, exactly as section 6 already requires:

        centre  =  median(x)

        spread  =  ( Q3(x) - Q1(x) ) / 1.3489795003921634

    where Q1 and Q3 are the 25th and 75th percentiles under numpy's default
    linear interpolation (numpy.percentile, method="linear"), computed after
    imputation and over the training rows only.

    THE DIVISOR IS THE NORMAL-CONSISTENCY CONSTANT, 2 * PHI^-1(0.75) =
    1.3489795003921634. It is what makes this a drop-in estimator of the same
    quantity rather than a change of units: for a Gaussian sample the
    interquartile range is 1.349 sigma in expectation, so dividing by that
    constant returns an estimate of sigma. A clean column is therefore scaled
    the same way it was before, and only an outlier-contaminated one moves.

    Without the divisor the qualifying columns would enter the penalised fit
    on a systematically different scale from every other column, and ridge
    penalises all columns equally - so an uncalibrated robust scale would
    silently change the effective penalty on those three columns and confound
    the very comparison this amendment exists to make.

    DEGENERATE CASE. If spread == 0 the column is treated as constant and the
    scale is set to 1.0, which is the rule the existing pipeline already
    applies when the standard deviation is zero. Unchanged in kind.

    IMPUTATION. A missing value in a qualifying column is filled with the
    training-row MEDIAN of that column rather than its mean, for the same
    reason the scale moved: the mean of a column containing 47.26 is not a
    typical value of it. Where the whole training column is missing the fill
    is 0.0, which is the existing guard, unchanged.

    MAD WAS THE ALTERNATIVE and IQR is chosen over it for one reason: the
    contamination is one-sided - a handful of very large lambdas on short
    windows, with no corresponding small tail - and the IQR uses both
    quartiles rather than folding the distribution about its median. Either
    would work. The choice is declared here and is not revisited after the
    result.

A4.3  THE ALTERNATIVE THAT WAS CONSIDERED AND REJECTED

    FIT THE SCALER ON ROWS CLEARING THE 380 BURN-IN, WHILE TRAINING ON ALL
    ROWS. Rejected, and not on grounds of taste:

        FOLD 1 HAS ZERO QUALIFYING ROWS. Its training set is 2021-22 entire,
        and every match of that season is fitted on a window below 380 -
        that is A2.2's finding, re-measured on D2's own training rows and
        reported as 380 of 380 in the contamination table.

        So the rule is UNDEFINED at fold 1. Making it defined means dropping
        fold 1 or redefining it, which changes the frozen fold structure -
        precisely the step A2.2 refused for the ladder and section 9 called
        serious.

    ALSO REJECTED: propagating the burn-in gate to D2's training rows, i.e.
    training only on rows clearing 380. Same defect, same reason: it costs
    fold 1 its entire training set. A2.2 already ruled this does not
    propagate, and this amendment does not reopen it.

    THE DEFECT IS IN THE SCALE, NOT THE TRAINING SET. A median-based scale is
    barely moved by a handful of 47.26s, so all four frozen folds stay in
    scope and no row is discarded. That is the whole reason to prefer it.

A4.4  WHAT THIS AMENDMENT IS NOT - AND THE DISTINCTION THAT MATTERS

    IT IS NOT A CHANGE TO SECTION 6. Section 6's rule - continuous features
    standardised on training statistics, booleans and indicators passing
    through - is unchanged for every column, including the three qualifying
    ones, which remain standardised continuous features. Only the estimator
    of the scale changes, and only where the source criterion of A4.1 holds.

    THE DISTINCTION FROM CHANGING SECTION 6 ITSELF, recorded here so the two
    can never be conflated later:

        The D0/D1/D2 sensitivity showed that standardising the boolean and
        indicator columns - which section 6 forbids - scores BETTER, by
        0.0035 at D1 and 0.0027 at D2, and that under it D2 reaches 0.9981
        and beats Elo v1.

        THAT CHANGE IS NOT MADE, and will not be made. There is a principled
        argument for it - ridge's penalty should be scale-invariant, and an
        unstandardised column has an arbitrary effective penalty - but the
        argument existed before the numbers and was not made. Adopting it now
        would be changing a declared rule because its alternative scored
        better.

        AMENDMENT 4 IS THE OPPOSITE CASE. It fixes a MEASURED DEFECT in how
        one specific quantity was estimated, identified by a contamination
        table rather than by a scoreboard, on columns selected by their
        source rather than by their result. The two are different in kind and
        section 6 stays as written through D3 and D4.

A4.5  WHAT IS RE-RUN, AND WHAT IS NOT

    D2 IS RE-FITTED under this amendment - same 88 features, same design
    width 92, same four frozen folds, same 21-point grid, same inner CV, same
    tie-break, same bootstrap seed. Only the scaling of three columns moves.

    D1 IS NOT RE-FITTED and does not change. It carries no qualifying column,
    so this amendment cannot touch it. The rescaled rung is compared against
    the SAME D1 the first run produced, and that identity is asserted rather
    than assumed.

    D0 IS NOT RE-FITTED. It has no design matrix.

    G6 AND THE FULL DS1-DS12 SUITE RE-RUN against the rescaled rung. A rung
    with an inherited leakage suite is not a result.

    THE ORIGINAL D2 ARTEFACTS ARE NOT OVERWRITTEN. The rescaled rung writes
    to its own filenames, so both are on disk and under the manifest, and the
    amendment can be given a size rather than replacing the thing it changed.

A4.6  A PREDICTION, RECORDED NOW SO IT CANNOT BE RETROFITTED

    If the compression is what silenced them, then under robust scaling
    rel_defence_diff and expected_total_goals MOVE OFF ZERO and the rescaled
    D2 improves on the original.

    IF THEY STAY AT ZERO ON A SANE SCALE, THEY GENUINELY CARRY LITTLE, and
    that is a clean finding rather than a failed amendment. It would mean the
    dynamic-state block is a two-column block on its merits - rel_elo_diff
    and rel_attack_diff - and that expected_total_goals does not do the job
    section 2 argued was load-bearing for it.

    Either outcome is reported. Neither licenses a further amendment to the
    scaling.

    rel_elo_diff's own coefficient may move even though its scaling does not,
    because the joint fit and the selected lambda can both shift. That is
    expected and is not evidence of anything.

===============================================================================
AMENDMENT 5 - D2-STATIC: THE IN-LADDER ANALOGUE OF TIER 2's STALE ARM
Written before D2-static is fitted. A new rung, declared in full.
===============================================================================

A5.0  WHY THIS RUNG EXISTS

    The D0/D1/D2 report set D2 - D1 against tier 2's Delta_recency of
    +0.0356 and read a shortfall. THAT COMPARISON WAS MIS-SPECIFIED.

    Tier 2 measured FRESH DC minus STALE DC. D1 is not the stale arm: its 84
    features include season-to-date points per game, last-5 form and venue
    form, every one of which updates match by match. D1 ALREADY CARRIES
    RECENCY, expressed as form rather than as a rating, so D2 - D1 was never
    the same quantity as A - C.

    Placing D1 and D2 against arm A instead puts them at 63% and 71% of the
    A-to-C gap. But that comparison crosses model families - a penalised
    multinomial logistic against a Dixon-Coles score matrix - so the absolute
    log losses are not strictly commensurable and it is recorded as an
    order-of-magnitude reframing, NOT as a fourth arm of tier 2.

    D2-STATIC REMOVES THE CAVEAT. Same features, same folds, same grid, same
    model family, same scaling. The single difference is that the dynamic
    state is frozen rather than refit. D2 - D2static is therefore the recency
    contribution measured in the ladder's own units, and it is the rung that
    should have answered the tier-2 question in the first place.

A5.1  THE FREEZING RULE - INHERITED, NOT INVENTED

    DIXON-COLES. One fit per SEASON, in place of one fit per distinct date.

        For a season S:
            cutoff  =  the maximum date in the window (Phase 2's own
                       convention for a static fit, and the reference the
                       107-day time decay is measured back from)
            window  =  every match with date STRICTLY EARLIER than the
                       first date of S
            state   =  attack, defence, home_multiplier and rho from that
                       single fit, used for EVERY match in S

    This is read out of phase4_tier2_window.run_fold_arm() under ARM_STATIC,
    which fits `train` at `window["date"].max()` and predicts the whole test
    season from it - and out of Phase 2's dc_static variant, which is the
    same rule. It is not a new cadence invented for this rung.

    IT COINCIDES WITH ARM A ON EVERY TEST ROW BY CONSTRUCTION: a fold's test
    season is preceded by exactly that fold's training seasons, so the window
    above IS arm A's window and the cutoff IS arm A's cutoff. G8 asserts this
    rather than claiming it - the generated static state must reproduce Phase
    2's stored dc_static lambdas on all 1,520 test rows.

    ELO. The rating each team carries at the START of its season.

        continuing team          1500 + (r_end_previous - 1500) * 0.75
        promoted, new to the
        dataset, or returning
        after an absence         1500 flat

    This is Phase 2's season_start_ratings() verbatim, the same policy
    section 6 already inherits, and it is READ from the frozen Elo artefact
    as each team's elo_before at its first match of the season rather than
    recomputed. Within a season the value never moves.

    PROMOTED AND ABSENT TEAMS UNDER THE DC FIT are handled exactly as
    match_rates() already handles them: a team absent from the window takes
    NEUTRAL_STRENGTH = 1.0. Inherited, unchanged, and the affected rows are
    counted and reported rather than absorbed.

A5.2  2021-22 HAS NO FROZEN STATE AT ALL, AND FOLD 1 IS THEREFORE DEGENERATE

    Stated in advance because it falls straight out of A5.1 and would
    otherwise look like a defect discovered afterwards.

    2021-22 is the first season in the dataset. Nothing precedes it, so its
    DC window is EMPTY and there is no fit: all three DC-derived columns are
    missing for all 380 of its matches. Every team in it is
    first_season_in_dataset, so every season-start rating is 1500 flat and
    rel_elo_diff_static is identically 0 across the whole season.

    FOLD 1 TRAINS ON 2021-22 ALONE. So under D2-static all four dynamic
    columns are constant in fold 1's training rows, the pipeline's existing
    constant-column rule renders them inert, and the ridge penalty drives
    their coefficients to zero.

    THE PREDICTION THAT FOLLOWS, recorded now and asserted as G9:

        D2-STATIC AT FOLD 1 MUST EQUAL D1 AT FOLD 1, BIT FOR BIT, and must
        select the same lambda.

    If it does not, something is wrong with the constant-column path and the
    rung is not readable. This is not a caveat being pre-excused; it is a
    checkable consequence being used as a test.

    IT ALSO MEANS D2 - D2static AT FOLD 1 EQUALS D2 - D1 AT FOLD 1. That is
    coherent rather than awkward: at fold 1 there is no frozen state to be
    had, so the whole of the block's contribution is recency.

    REPORTING RULE, DECLARED BEFORE THE RESULT: D2 - D2static is reported
    pooled over all four folds AND over folds 2-4, where the DC block is
    genuinely present in the training rows. Both are reported every time.
    Neither is selected after the fact, and the four-fold figure is primary.

A5.3  EVERYTHING ELSE IS HELD

    Same 88 features and design width 92. Same four frozen outer folds and
    1,520 outer test matches. Same 21-point grid, selected per fold. Same
    5-segment, 4-fold inner CV with ties to the smallest. Same solver and
    tolerance. Same bootstrap: 10,000 draws, seed 20260901. Same section 6
    rule, and the same Amendment 4 robust scaling on the same three columns,
    so D2 - D2static is never a comparison between two scaling rules.

    G6 and the full DS1-DS12 suite run against this rung as against any
    other.

A5.4  WHAT D2 - D2static WILL AND WILL NOT LICENSE

    It measures, within one model family and on one set of folds, what
    refitting the dynamic state per calendar date buys over freezing it at
    season start.

    It is placed alongside tier 2's Delta_recency of +0.0356 as the
    like-for-like measurement that comparison was reaching for. It does NOT
    inherit tier 2's interval, it is not a re-run of tier 2, and agreement
    between the two numbers would not make them the same experiment.

    It does not license any causal claim, and a non-significant difference
    does not prove that freezing costs nothing.

END OF AMENDMENT 5

0.  WHAT THIS RUNG IS FOR
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The tier-2 experiment returned WITHIN-SEASON UPDATING:

        Delta_total    +0.0359  [+0.0186, +0.0535]
        Delta_recency  +0.0356  [+0.0177, +0.0533]

1.  REFIT CADENCE - INHERITED, NOT INVENTED  (load-bearing)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Read out of phase2_poisson_dixon_coles.run_fold():

        ONE REFIT PER DISTINCT MATCH DATE.
        The fitting window is every match with date STRICTLY < that date.

    Not per gameweek, not weekly, not per match. Per distinct calendar date,
    strictly prior. 583 distinct dates across the 1,900 matches; 582 refits
    (the first date has no prior window).

    This is stated at this length because recency is 99% of the effect, so
    cadence is not an implementation detail. It is held FIXED across every
    rung D2-D4 and across the D2-DC diagnostic.

    Already implemented and verified in scripts/phase4_dc_passthrough.py:
    G1 asserts the generated state reproduces Phase 2's stored test-row
    lambda_home / lambda_away to 0.000e+00 on all 1,520 test rows.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.  FEATURES - DIFFERENCES ONLY, FOUR COLUMNS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    rel_elo_diff           home Elo before match  -  away Elo before match
    rel_attack_diff        DC attack, refit per window, home - away
    rel_defence_diff       DC defence, refit per window, home - away
    expected_total_goals   lambda_home + lambda_away

    expected_total_goals is load-bearing and is NOT dropped for parsimony.
    Without it D2 cannot represent scoring ENVIRONMENT - the quantity that
    moves draw probability independently of the mismatch between the sides -
    and would trail DC for a reason unrelated to dynamic state.

    NOT INCLUDED, deliberately:
        home_elo_before / away_elo_before alongside rel_elo_diff. Levels plus
        their own difference is the redundancy this project removed earlier.
        The difference alone is kept.

    Dixon-Coles is the PRIMARY state source and Elo secondary: DC's advantage
    is recency-driven and the 107-day half-life is doing that work, whereas
    Elo's K = 20 is a coarser recency mechanism over the same information.

4.  INNER VALIDATION - AND A CONFLICT IN THE PHASE 4 PRE-DECLARATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Section 7 of outputs/phase4_dynamic_state_predeclaration.txt requires BOTH

        (a) blocks defined as contiguous (season, matchweek) blocks, and
        (b) every validation date STRICTLY later than every training date,
            asserted programmatically as DS12

    THESE ARE INCOMPATIBLE IN THIS DATASET and DS12 would fail. This is not a
    new discovery; it is Instrument 4's Amendment 1, re-measured here on the
    Phase 4 training rows:

        (season, matchweek) training blocks spanning over 7 days:  91
        worst case: 2022-23 matchweek 8 spans 2022-09-16 to 2023-04-04

    Postponed fixtures keep their ORIGINAL matchweek number, so a matchweek is
    not an interval of time and blocks built from it overlap in date.

    RESOLUTION, following Amendment 1: the atomic unit is the CALENDAR DATE.
    Everything else in section 7 is kept exactly:

        blocks         5 contiguous chronological segments of the outer
                       training rows, approximately equal match count
        inner folds    4, expanding: fold i trains on blocks 1..i and
                       validates on block i+1
        constraint     max(train date) < min(validation date), strictly,
                       asserted as DS12
        statistic      mean validation log loss pooled across the 4 folds
        tie-break      SMALLEST lambda among ties
                       (note: Phase 3 used LARGEST. Phase 4's rule governs.)

    FOLD 1'S 380 TRAINING MATCHES: the same construction applies unchanged -
    5 segments of roughly 76 matches, 4 expanding inner folds validating on
    roughly 76 matches each, pooling ~304 validation matches. No special case,
    because a scheme that changed shape at fold 1 would confound "lambda
    differs by fold" with "the selector differs by fold".

9.  THE OPEN DECISION - WHY THIS DOCUMENT IS BLOCKED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The DC-passthrough diagnostic ran and returned INVALID on a regime gate,
    not on the link. The finding:

        Phase 2's walk-forward only ever fits windows of at least one full
        training season, and only ever PREDICTS test-season matches. Applying
        the same rule to TRAINING rows enters a regime Phase 2 never did.

        lambda_home reaches 47.26 on a 19-match window (West Ham, 2021-08-23)
        and 21.94 on a 20-match window, against a maximum of 4.64 across all
        1,520 test rows. Dixon-Coles strengths are not identified on a
        fortnight of football.

        Those outliers sit in the TRAINING rows, so they distort the
        standardiser the test rows are then mapped through. That is what
        produced fold 2's passthrough log loss of 27.09 - not the logistic
        link. Reading that number as a link measurement would have been a
        false finding that reshaped the whole Phase 4 design.

        Exactly 380 training rows - the whole of 2021-22 - are fitted on
        windows smaller than the smallest window any test row was scored on.

    THE DECISION REQUIRED, and it must be made before any fit:

        A. BURN-IN BY SEASON. D2's training rows begin at 2022-23; 2021-22
           supplies state but is never a training row. Costs fold 1 its entire
           training set, so fold 1 would have to be dropped or redefined -
           which changes the frozen fold structure and is therefore a serious
           step.

        B. MINIMUM-WINDOW THRESHOLD. Training rows require a fitting window of
           at least N matches. N must be declared before fitting, not chosen
           from the failure. A principled, untuned choice is N = 380, one full
           season, which is also the smallest window any test row has - it
           makes training and test regimes identical by construction.

        C. WINSORISE / CLIP the state features at bounds fitted on training
           rows only. Keeps every row but changes the feature definition, and
           the bound is a new free parameter.

        D. Something else.

    RECOMMENDATION: B with N = 380. It is the only option whose threshold is
    fixed by the experiment's own structure rather than by the observed
    failure - it equalises the training and test regimes by definition - and
    it keeps all four frozen folds intact, which A does not.

    NOT CHOSEN HERE. Choosing a burn-in threshold having already seen which
    value broke fold 2 is precisely what this project's pre-declarations exist
    to prevent. The decision is recorded as an amendment before anything is
    fitted.

===============================================================================
AMENDMENT 2 - BURN-IN THRESHOLD, AND AN EXTENDED LAMBDA GRID
Written before the re-run. Section 9 is unblocked by this.
===============================================================================

A2.1  THE BURN-IN THRESHOLD IS 380, AND IT IS DERIVED, NOT TUNED

    G7 requires every training row to come from a fitting window at least as
    large as the smallest window any test row was scored on.

    That smallest window is 380 BY CONSTRUCTION, and the derivation needs no
    data at all:

        test rows are only ever scored inside a test season;
        every test season is preceded by at least one complete season;
        a complete season is 380 matches;
        therefore no test row is ever scored on a window below 380.

    From which the threshold follows as a fixed point, not a choice:

        below 380  admits training rows fitted on evidence no test row
                   ever sees - the model would be taught in a regime it is
                   never used in
        above 380  discards training rows whose regime the test set
                   genuinely contains - throwing away valid evidence

    380 is the only value that makes the training and test regimes identical.
    It was derivable before anything ran, and this amendment asserts it from
    the structure above rather than from any observed number.

    ON 47.26, AND WHY IT IS NOT AN INPUT HERE

    lambda_home reaching 47.26 on a 19-match window is the observation that
    prompted G7 to EXIST. It is cited for that, and for nothing else. It is
    not an input to the threshold's value, and the threshold would be 380 had
    the largest early lambda been 5 or 500.

    That distinction is the entire difference between this amendment and the
    pattern Phase 3's Amendment 2 fell into, where a rule was changed after
    the result was known and the outcome moved with it. Here the failure
    prompted a gate; the gate's threshold comes from the fold structure.

A2.2  THE COST, STATED PLAINLY

    Under the gate, 2021-22 contributes NO training rows - all 380 of its
    matches are fitted on windows below 380, most of them far below.

    Fold 1 trains on 2021-22 alone. So fold 1 has no training set under the
    gate and the DC-PASSTHROUGH DIAGNOSTIC RUNS ON FOLDS 2, 3 AND 4 ONLY.

    That is acceptable for this diagnostic and only this one: it answers a
    yes/no question about whether the logistic link preserves Dixon-Coles, and
    three folds and 1,140 test matches answer it.

    IT DOES NOT PROPAGATE TO D2. All four frozen outer folds remain in force
    for the ladder. If the burn-in gate turns out to bind on D2's own training
    rows, that is a separate question requiring its own declaration - it is
    NOT inherited from this amendment.

    CONSEQUENCE FOR THE COMPARISON FIGURE: dc_walkforward's 0.9904 is a
    four-fold number. A three-fold passthrough may not be compared against it.
    DC's figure is recomputed on folds 2-4 only, and every delta and interval
    in the re-run is against that recomputed figure.

A2.3  THE LAMBDA GRID IS EXTENDED DOWNWARD - DECLARED BEFORE THE RE-RUN

    Two of four folds selected lambda at the grid floor of 0.01. A floor
    selection means the grid was wrong for this feature regime, and Phase 4
    section 8 requires it be reported rather than absorbed.

    This is independent of the window problem and is fixed independently.

    NEW GRID, 21 points, half-decade spacing throughout, extending the
    existing 13 downward by four decades:

        0.000001, 0.000003, 0.00001, 0.00003, 0.0001, 0.0003,
        0.001,    0.003,
        0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, 10, 30, 100, 300, 1000, 3000, 10000

    The upper 13 are unchanged, so any earlier selection remains
    representable and the two grids agree wherever they overlap.

    A PREDICTION, RECORDED NOW SO IT CANNOT BE RETROFITTED

    The passthrough design carries exactly TWO features against 760 or more
    training rows. That is a regime in which the unpenalised maximum-likelihood
    fit is well posed and essentially optimal, so the inner CV has little
    reason to prefer any positive penalty. The floor may therefore continue to
    bind however far the grid is extended, and if it does that is NOT the
    pathology G6 was written to catch - it is the correct answer for p = 2.

    G6 is nevertheless left as a hard FAIL rather than softened. Per the
    brief, a floor selection after the G7 gate is a STOP-and-report, and the
    reading above is offered as an interpretation for that report, not as a
    licence to continue.

A2.4  SECTION 9 IS UNBLOCKED

    Option B is adopted at N = 380. Sections 1-8 are unchanged. Nothing else
    in this document moves.

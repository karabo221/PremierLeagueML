WHY THIS IS TWO FINDINGS, NOT ONE

            The rung has an IMPLEMENTATION defect and a FRAMING error, and
            they are independent. Fixing the scaler would produce a rung
            that runs cleanly and still answers a narrower question than

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  THE DEFECT: THE DEGENERATE-COLUMN GUARD FIRING ON COLUMNS THAT VARY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A6.1  WHAT G10 CAUGHT

      G10 asserts that no column takes the zero-scale guard while actually
      varying. The guard replaces a computed scale of zero with 1.0, on the
      reasoning that a column with no spread is CONSTANT and scaling a
      constant is meaningless. Where the column is not constant, the guard
      instead hands a genuinely varying column to a penalised fit in RAW
      units - the same class of defect Amendment 4 exists to remove.

      OBSERVED: 2 offenders, both at D2-static fold 2 -

          rel_attack_diff        scale 1.0, training values not all equal
          rel_defence_diff       scale 1.0, training values not all equal

      D2 rescaled has ZERO offenders at every fold. The defect is specific
      to the static rung.

A6.2  THE CAUSE, IN FULL

      The static rung freezes DC state at season start (A5.1). 2021-22 is
      the first season in the data, so it has no prior season to freeze
      from and every one of its 380 rows carries NO DC STATE AT ALL.

      Those rows are imputed to the TRAINING MEDIAN, which is the
      pipeline's declared rule and is not itself the defect.

      2021-22's share of each fold's training rows, measured, not inferred
      (audit rows G10b-D2_static in outputs/phase4_a4_audit.csv):

          fold 1      100%     trains on 2021-22 alone
          fold 2       50%     2021-22 of 2021-22 + 2022-23
          fold 3       33%
          fold 4       25%

      AT EXACTLY 50% THE ARITHMETIC IS FORCED. Half the training rows hold
      one identical imputed value - the median. The 25th and 75th
      percentiles therefore BOTH land on that value, the interquartile
      range is identically zero, and Amendment 4's robust scale IQR/1.34898
      is identically zero. The guard fires. This is not a numerical
      accident that a tolerance would absorb; it is what the median does to
      the quartiles when it occupies half the mass.

      Fold 1 is a different case and G10 correctly does NOT flag it. There
      all four columns are genuinely constant, so a scale of 1.0 is the
      right answer for the right reason.

A6.3  THE THIRD COLUMN, WHICH THE GUARD DID NOT CATCH

      expected_total_goals at fold 2 did not take the guard, because its
      imputed median does not sit exactly on both quartiles. It got
      something worse, because it passed silently:

          D2-static  fold 2   train_scale   0.001083   (centre 2.697387)
          D2 rescaled fold 2  train_scale   0.690275

      The same column, the same fold, divided by a scale 637x smaller. Its
      standardised values enter the penalised fit inflated by that factor
      against every other column in the design, and no gate fired.

      A NOTE ON THE MULTIPLIER. The brief that commissioned this amendment
      put the inflation at "~400x". The measured ratio against D2
      rescaled's scale on the same column and fold is 637x. The figure
      depends on which scale is taken as the reference, so the two are not
      in conflict about the defect - only about the denominator. The
      measured number is recorded here rather than the remembered one.

      Folds 3 and 4 are compressed too, at 2.0x to 3.0x, and are not
      flagged by anything. The defect is a GRADIENT, not a fold-2 event;
      fold 2 is only where it crosses the threshold that makes it visible.

A6.4  WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT IMPLY

      It does NOT imply the D2-static numbers are slightly off. Two columns
      entering a penalised fit in raw units and a third inflated 637x means
      the rung's coefficients are not on a common scale, and a ridge penalty
      is not scale-invariant. THE NUMBERS ARE NOT READABLE. That is why
      they are marked so rather than reported with a caveat.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.  THE FRAMING ERROR, WHICH IS MINE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A6.5  WHAT THE RUNG WAS COMMISSIONED TO ANSWER

      D2-static was commissioned to give the RECENCY contribution in the
      ladder's own units, as a within-family replacement for the tier-2
      comparison's 0.0356 - which crosses model families and carries that
      caveat.

A6.6  WHY IT CANNOT ANSWER THAT, AS DESIGNED

      D1's 84 form features UPDATE MATCH BY MATCH IN BOTH ARMS. Only the
      four dynamic-state columns freeze. So:

          D2 - D2static  =  the MARGINAL recency of the RATING, on top of
                            form features that are already fresh in both
                            arms

      That is a real quantity. It is not the tier-2 quantity, which
      compares a fresh model against a genuinely stale one. The tier-2
      comparison freezes everything; this rung freezes four columns out of
      eighty-eight and leaves the rest current.

      IT COULD NEVER HAVE APPROACHED 0.0356, and no result it returned
      would have been evidence about that number in either direction. A
      small D2 - D2static would not have contradicted tier 2, and a large
      one would not have confirmed it.

A6.7  WHOSE ERROR THIS IS

      MINE, and it is a FRAMING error, not an implementation one. The rung
      that was specified is the rung that was built: identical features,
      folds, grid, family and scaling, with the state frozen at season
      start. A5.1 through A5.3 describe it accurately and it implements
      them accurately.

      The error is that I declared the resulting difference would answer
      the tier-2 question in the ladder's units. It does not, and the
      reason was visible in the design before anything was fitted. This is
      recorded against the brief, not against the code.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.  THE ONLY DESIGN THAT WOULD MAKE THE RUNG READABLE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A6.8  THE RESTRICTION

      Restrict BOTH arms' TRAINING rows to seasons that carry frozen state
      at all - 2022-23 onward - and run FOLDS 2, 3 AND 4 ONLY. Fold 1
      trains on 2021-22 alone and would have no training rows left.

      This removes the imputed mass that drives A6.1 through A6.3 at its
      source, rather than adding a guard or a tolerance downstream of it.
      A6.2's arithmetic is forced by the imputation share; delete the share
      and the arithmetic does not arise.

A6.9  THE PAIRING REQUIREMENT, WHICH IS THE PART EASIEST TO GET WRONG

      D2 MUST BE RE-RUN ON THE SAME RESTRICTED TRAINING SET.

      COMPARING A RESTRICTED D2-STATIC AGAINST THE EXISTING FULL-TRAINING
      D2 IS FORBIDDEN. It would confound the thing being measured (state
      cadence) with a thing that is not (training-set size), and the two
      move the same direction, so the confound would inflate rather than
      cancel. The resulting number would look like a recency effect and
      would be partly a sample-size effect, with nothing in the output to
      separate them.

      This is stated as a prohibition rather than a caution because it is
      the cheap path and it produces a publishable-looking number.

A6.10 WHAT THE RESTRICTED RUNG WOULD AND WOULD NOT FIX

      It fixes the SCALER defect. It does NOT fix the framing error of
      A6.6: form features stay fresh in both arms whatever the training
      restriction is. The restricted rung answers A6.6's marginal-recency
      question CLEANLY. It still does not answer the tier-2 question.

      Answering that would need both arms' FORM FEATURES frozen too, which
      is a different rung again and is not the one that was declared.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.  THE DEFERRAL, AND ITS COST
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A6.11 DEFERRED, DELIBERATELY

      The restricted rung is not run in this session and is not scheduled.

A6.12 WHAT IS GIVEN UP BY DEFERRING IT

      It would decompose the D2 - D1 effect of 0.0037 into two components:

          RATING-INFORMATION    what the DC and Elo state contribute at all
          RATING-RECENCY        what is contributed specifically by
                                updating that state per calendar date
                                rather than per season

      That decomposition is REAL and it is not obtainable any other way in
      the ladder's units.

      IT IS ALSO SMALL. The whole effect being decomposed is 0.0037, so
      both components are bounded well below it, and the rung sits outside
      the declared D0-D4 ladder. Three folds rather than four, on a
      reduced training set, would put wide intervals on both halves of a
      small number.

      The stakes are stated plainly here so that deferring it is a decision
      on the record rather than an omission - and so that a later session
      picking it up knows it is buying a decomposition of 0.0037 and not a
      headline.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.  WHAT THIS AMENDMENT DOES NOT DO
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A6.13 G10 IS NOT SOFTENED AND NO TOLERANCE IS ADDED

      G10 carries no tunable constant. It tests an implication the pipeline
      already relies on - that the guard's "this column is constant" is
      true when the guard fires - and it fails exactly when that
      implication is false. It would read identically had every rung
      passed. It STAYS FAILING until D2-static is rebuilt per section 3.

A6.14 G9 IS NOT TOUCHED

      G9 fails at 2.220e-16, from a 279x279 Newton system against a
      267x267 one. That is a correct test of an over-strong claim in A5.2,
      the same class as C3a/C3b, and A5.2 is NOT softened. G9b separately
      records that the MECHANISM A5.2 predicted did hold exactly - every
      dynamic-state coefficient at fold 1 is 0.0 - so the strict form
      failing is not reported as the mechanism failing. Both rows stand as
      they are.

A6.15 NO D2-STATIC NUMBER IS QUOTED

      The scales in A6.3 are properties of the BROKEN SCALER and are the
      evidence for the defect. No log loss, RPS, Brier, accuracy, delta or
      confidence interval from the D2-static rung appears in this document
      or anywhere else.

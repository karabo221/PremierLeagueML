-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0.  WHAT THIS IS FOR
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

E0.1  THE TARGET, SIZED AGAINST WHAT PHASE 4 COULD SEE:

          Dixon-Coles -> market        0.02979    what Phase 5 competes for
          D1 -> Dixon-Coles            0.01357    the whole Phase 4 rating gap
          D2 - D1                      0.00365    Phase 4's largest effect

      Resolution on 1,520 matches is roughly 0.005. The target is 2.2x the
      entire Phase 4 rating gap, so unlike most Phase 4 blocks an effect
      here is detectable if it is there.

E0.2  WHY THIS RUNS BEFORE THE xG ARM. Shots on target is a cruder version
      of what xG measures - shots weighted by quality - so SoT-based
      ratings are a LOWER BOUND on xG's information. E1 sizes A.

E0.3  ONE RESPECT IN WHICH E1 IS A BETTER BOUND THAN IT LOOKS. The xG
      pre-declaration records an untestable leak: providers refit their xG
      models, so today's xG for a 2021 match may come from a model trained
      through 2026 (A4.2). SHOT COUNTS HAVE NO SUCH PROBLEM. They were
      recorded contemporaneously and no model produced them. E1's estimate
      is therefore clean in a way the xG arm's cannot be, and a positive E1
      is stronger evidence for A than a positive A would be for itself.

1.  THE DATA, AND WHAT USING IT DOES NOT LICENSE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

E1.1  The shot columns arrive in the football-data.co.uk files already
      frozen for Phase 5B: HS, AS, HST, AST, HC, AC, HF, AF, HY, AY, HR,
      AR, on all 1,900 matches, joined 1,900/1,900 with zero unmatched and
      zero scoreline or date disagreements. No download and no scrape.

E1.2  THE ODDS COLUMNS IN THOSE SAME FILES REMAIN BENCHMARK-ONLY. They
      never enter a design matrix, a rating window, or a feature. That a
      file is now read for two purposes does not merge the purposes, and a
      gate asserts it - E9.

E1.3  THE FILES DO NOT CHANGE. They are under FROZEN_MANIFEST.txt at the
      hashes Phase 5B recorded. This instrument re-reads them; it does not
      re-download them.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.  THE DATA AUDIT, RUN BEFORE THIS DESIGN WAS FIXED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

E2.1  MISSINGNESS: ZERO. All twelve columns, all five seasons, all 1,900
      matches. Nothing to impute and no coverage gate to fail - which is
      the opposite of what Phase 5B found for Pinnacle, and it is why
      there is no primary/sensitivity split here.

E2.2  PER-SEASON MEANS - no definitional discontinuity:

          season      HS      AS     HST     AST      HC      AC
          2021-22   13.85   11.74   4.68    4.14    5.60    4.82
          2022-23   13.95   11.31   4.91    3.90    5.64    4.47
          2023-24   15.40   12.18   5.44    4.43    6.12    4.71
          2024-25   13.75   12.17   4.83    4.27    5.43    4.87
          2025-26   13.84   11.16   4.50    3.87    5.40    4.60

      2023-24 sits high on every shot column at once and returns to trend
      afterwards. A DEFINITIONAL change would not revert. This is recorded
      as a season effect and is NOT adjusted for; adjusting it would be
      fitting the source to the expectation.

E2.3  GOALS PER SHOT ON TARGET IS REMARKABLY STABLE, which matters
      directly for section 4's conversion step:

          2021-22  0.3195    2022-23  0.3241    2023-24  0.3323
          2024-25  0.3224    2025-26  0.3284

      A spread of 0.013 across five seasons. The quantity section 4 needs
      to estimate is not a volatile one.

E2.4  ONE IMPOSSIBLE ROW, REPORTED AND NOT REPAIRED:

          2021-08-15  Newcastle 2-4 West Ham   AS = 8, AST = 9

      Shots on target exceed shots. It is a source error. It is left
      exactly as it is, and E4 asserts the count is exactly one - so a
      change in the source is detected rather than absorbed.

E2.5  TWELVE ROWS WHERE GOALS EXCEED SHOTS ON TARGET, WHICH ARE PROBABLY
      NOT ERRORS. An own goal is not credited as a shot on target to the
      side that benefits, so goals > SoT is the EXPECTED signature of an
      own goal, not of corruption. Examples: Manchester City 5-0 Norwich
      with HST 4; Crystal Palace 1-1 Newcastle with AST 0.

      THIS IS STATED AS THE LIKELY EXPLANATION AND NOT AS A VERIFIED ONE -
      nothing in this dataset records own goals, so it cannot be checked
      here. E4 asserts the count is exactly twelve for the same reason as
      E2.4.

E2.6  WHY NEITHER FINDING BLOCKS THE RUNG. The estimator of section 3
      consumes weighted SUMS over a window of hundreds of matches. One
      unit of error in one match of 1,900 cannot move a fold-level result.
      The rows are reported because a source error that is known is
      different from a source error that is absorbed, not because they
      threaten the estimate.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.  E1a - THE ESTIMATOR, AND THE ONE THING THAT CHANGES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

E3.1  fit_attack_defence() IS REUSED UNCHANGED. No second estimator is
      written. It is multiplicative iterative scaling, the closed-form
      coordinate solution of the weighted Poisson likelihood, and its
      inputs enter only as

          scored[t]   = sum of weights * (SoT by t)
          conceded[t] = sum of weights * (SoT against t)

      Sums. Nothing requires integers, and SoT are integers anyway.

E3.2  rho IS FITTED ON ACTUAL GOALS IN BOTH ARMS. STATED EXPLICITLY HERE
      RATHER THAN INHERITED FROM THE xG DECLARATION.

      fit_rho() selects matches by (home_goals <= 1) & (away_goals <= 1)
      and corrects the four discrete cells 0-0, 1-0, 0-1, 1-1. Those are
      SCORELINE categories. "Both sides had at most one shot on target" is
      a different event - it is common, it is not low-scoring, and tau is
      not defined over it. A correction invented for an event Dixon-Coles
      does not describe would not be Dixon-Coles.

      So only attack, defence and the home multiplier come from SoT.

E3.3  EVERYTHING ELSE IS HELD IDENTICAL between the arms, and E2 asserts
      it field by field: 107-day half-life, one refit per distinct match
      date, window of matches with date STRICTLY EARLIER than the match
      predicted, same initialisation, same convergence settings, same
      MAX_GOALS = 25, same score matrix, same outcome_probabilities().

      If anything else differs, the comparison measures that instead.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.  THE LOAD-BEARING CHOICE:  HOW SoT RATES BECOME H/D/A
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

E4.1  CANDIDATE (i) IS CHOSEN. Scale the SoT lambdas to the goal scale by
      the training-window conversion rate, then use the existing
      outcome_probabilities() unchanged.

E4.2  WHY, AND THE REASON IS NOT CONVENIENCE.

      (a) IT IS THE ONLY ONE THAT BOUNDS THE xG ARM. The xG declaration
          commits to the Dixon-Coles architecture with rho on goals. If E1
          used a different architecture - SoT differences as ladder
          features - it would not be a lower bound on that experiment. It
          would be a different experiment that happens to use shots.

      (b) IT MAKES "E1a - Dixon-Coles" A RATING-VERSUS-RATING COMPARISON.
          Two models identical in every respect except what the rating is
          estimated from. That is the claim being tested, and candidate
          (ii) cannot express it: a ladder rung against Dixon-Coles
          confounds the rating input with the whole logistic apparatus.

      (c) Candidate (ii) is not discarded - it is E1b's territory, and E1b
          runs second by the pre-committed gate order.

E4.3  THE CONVERSION RATE, ESTIMATED ON TRAINING ROWS ONLY.

          c  =  ( sum of weights * total goals in window )
                / ( sum of weights * total SoT in window )

      computed over THE SAME decayed window as the ratings, at the same
      refit dates, from matches strictly earlier than the match being
      predicted. It never sees the match it scales, and never sees an
      outer-test row. Both lambdas are multiplied by the same c.

E4.4  ONE CONSTANT, POOLED OVER HOME AND AWAY. The home multiplier is
      already fitted inside the SoT model, so a second home-specific
      conversion would be a second home-advantage parameter estimated from
      the same window.

      DECLARED SENSITIVITY, reported whatever it says: separate home and
      away conversion constants. The primary is the pooled one and does
      not change.

E4.5  E2.3 IS THE JUSTIFICATION FOR TREATING c AS ESTIMABLE. It moves
      0.013 across five seasons. If it had swung wildly, candidate (i)
      would have been the wrong choice and this section would say so.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.  THE FALSIFIABLE PREDICTION, IN A4.6's FORM
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

E5.1  THE MECHANISM CLAIMED: goals are a low-count realisation of a rate.
      A team's SoT count per match is roughly 4-5 against roughly 1.4
      goals, so SoT estimates the same underlying attacking rate with less
      sampling noise per match.

E5.2  IF THAT MECHANISM IS REAL, BOTH OF THESE FOLLOW:

      (a) E1a beats goals-Dixon-Coles on log loss, with RPS agreeing.
      (b) E1a's fitted attack and defence estimates are MORE STABLE across
          consecutive refits than goals-DC's, measured as the mean
          absolute change in a team's attack parameter between successive
          refit dates.

E5.3  IF ONLY (b) HOLDS AND NOT (a): SoT is estimating the rate more
      precisely and the rate is not what the extra information was needed
      for. That is a real and reportable finding, and it is the outcome
      that would most change how the xG arm is read - it would say the
      remaining 0.02979 is not a rating-precision problem at all.

E5.4  IF NEITHER HOLDS: the conversion constant has absorbed the
      difference and SoT is a noisier proxy for what goals already carry.
      Reported plainly, with no third arm added afterwards to rescue it.

E5.5  BOTH BRANCHES ARE WRITTEN HERE SO THAT NEITHER CAN BE PRESENTED AS
      THE ONE THAT WAS EXPECTED.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.  E1b - ROLLING SHOT RESIDUALS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

E6.1  THE SIGNAL: who has been finishing above or below their chances.
      Orthogonal to the rating by construction, because it is the
      DIFFERENCE between what the shots implied and what the scoreline
      recorded.

E6.2  WINDOW: THE LAST FIVE MATCHES, WITHIN SEASON. This is not a chosen
      parameter - it is the project's existing convention, matching D1's
      last5_pts_before and last5_mp_before. Introducing a new window
      length would be introducing a tuned one, and the tuning would happen
      on these outer-test rows.

E6.3  SEASON BOUNDARIES: the window NEVER crosses one. Phase 1's T2
      already asserts that current-season state does not carry across a
      season boundary, and these columns are built by the same rule and
      inherit the same gate.

E6.4  INSUFFICIENT HISTORY: a team with fewer than five prior matches in
      the current season gets NaN, imputed to the TRAINING MEDIAN by the
      pipeline's declared rule - the same rule every other column takes -
      accompanied by an availability indicator on the project's existing
      *_available convention. No back-filling from the prior season, which
      would violate E6.3.

E6.5  THE COLUMNS, six of them:

          home_sot_residual_last5     away_sot_residual_last5
          rel_sot_residual_diff
          home_sot_residual_available away_sot_residual_available
          rel_sot_residual_available

      where a team's residual is (its SoT differential over the window)
      minus (its goal differential over the window), both per match.

E6.6  SCALING: SECTION 6's STANDARD TREATMENT, NOT AMENDMENT 4's ROBUST
      SCALING. Amendment 4's robust rule applies to DC-DERIVED columns -
      it selects rel_attack_diff, rel_defence_diff and
      expected_total_goals BY NAME. These residuals come from a rolling
      arithmetic window and not from any fitted model, so they are not in
      that class. A4a continues to assert the robust mask still selects
      exactly three columns, which is what would catch this drifting.

      The indicators pass through unstandardised under section 6, as every
      indicator in this project does.

E6.7  E1b IS A LADDER BLOCK on D2 rescaled, so per-rung per-fold lambda,
      blocked inner CV on training rows only, and G6 all apply to it.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.  GATE ORDER, PRE-COMMITTED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

E7.1  E1a FIRST. THEN E1b ON WHICHEVER BASE E1a LEAVES STANDING.

      If E1a beats goals-DC significantly, E1b is built on the base that
      result implies. If E1a is null or inconclusive, E1b is built on D2
      rescaled. Written now so the base cannot be chosen after seeing
      which gives E1b the better number.

E7.2  A NOTE ON THE REPORT TEMPLATE. Lambda, EPV and G6 are penalised-
      logistic concepts. E1a is a standalone probability model in the
      Dixon-Coles family and HAS NO PENALISED FIT, so it has no lambda, no
      EPV and no G6 row. It reports the full Phase 0 metric set, rho, the
      conversion constant c, and the fitted attack/defence parameters.
      E1b reports lambda, EPV and G6 in full. This is a deviation from the
      requested template and is declared rather than silently applied.

9.  INTERPRETATION, DECLARED BEFORE RUNNING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

E9.1  E1 SIGNIFICANT      xG is very likely to add more, since SoT is a
                          crude version of what xG measures. The five
                          FBref downloads are justified.

E9.2  E1 NULL             shot information adds little over goals at this
                          resolution. xG's expected gain is small. A is
                          SCOPED ACCORDINGLY - not abandoned, not expected
                          to move much.

E9.3  E1 INCONCLUSIVE     reported as such, read neither way.

E9.4  THE STANDING RULES. No block is declared useful on accuracy. A
      non-significant difference is not equality - 1,520 matches did not
      separate Block C from nothing either, and that was not equality.
      RPS must agree in sign with log loss or the comparison is
      INCONCLUSIVE.

E9.5  NO NUMBER IN PHASES 0 TO 5B MOVES BECAUSE OF THIS INSTRUMENT.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. WHAT THIS CANNOT ESTABLISH
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

      It cannot establish what xG would do. It bounds it from below on an
      argument about information content, and that argument could be
      wrong: xG could carry something SoT does not in a way that changes
      the sign rather than the size.

      It cannot make the project's numbers out-of-sample. 2025-26 was
      scored during Phase 3's lambda sweep and its B0-B6 ablation. Every
      figure remains a walk-forward development estimate, optimistic by an
      unknown margin, and the market benchmark it is measured against is
      not.

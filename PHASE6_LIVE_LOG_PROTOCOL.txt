-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0.  WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS, AND WHAT IT HAS NO AUTHORITY OVER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

L0.1  WHY A LIVE LOG AT ALL. A season-end batch run ASSERTS that the freeze
      held. A pre-kickoff timestamped log PROVES what was said before the
      result existed. The two are different strengths of evidence and the
      second is the stronger one, because it does not depend on trusting the
      operator's account of the order in which things happened.

L0.2  IT CHANGES NOTHING. No model, no feature, no hyperparameter, no rating,
      no evaluation rule, no cutoff, no vocabulary. It reads the frozen
      instruments and writes down what they said. If this document is doing
      anything else, it is being misused.

L0.3  THE RULES BELOW BIND THE LOG ONLY. Nothing in this protocol may alter
      what the holdout scores, which matches it contains, or how it is
      measured. Those are fixed by the freeze and the pin, and where a rule
      here could be read as touching them - L2 is the live one - it is
      written explicitly not to.

L0.4  THE LOG IS NOT A RESULT AND IS NOT REPORTABLE AS ONE. See L5.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  L1 - CADENCE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

L1.1  ONE PREDICTION ROUND PER MATCHWEEK, written before that matchweek's
      first kickoff.

L1.2  THE STATE CUTOFF IS THE DAY BEFORE THE MATCHWEEK'S FIRST FIXTURE, and
      the rule is the walk-forward's strict rule, unrelaxed:

          state window  =  every completed match with date < first fixture

      "The day before, inclusive" and "strictly before the first fixture"
      select the same set of matches. The second is implemented because it
      needs no calendar arithmetic to be correct, and calendar arithmetic
      across a postponement is where an off-by-one would hide.

L1.3  THE ROUND BOUNDARY IS DERIVED, NOT LABELLED. Fixtures are taken in date
      order until a side would appear twice. That is the only property that is
      actually true of a matchweek, and it is used rather than a matchweek
      number because P7.2 of the pin deliberately does NOT carry matchweek -
      H3.4 makes the cutoff a date rule - and because the fixture feed
      publishes a midweek card alongside a weekend one without saying which
      is which.

      A matchweek_label is recorded alongside, derived from the completed
      count, and it is INFORMATIONAL. Nothing reads it.

L1.4  PREDICTIONS ARE NEVER REGENERATED ONCE WRITTEN. Enforced in code: the
      generator refuses to write a match_id already present in the log, and
      the refusal raises rather than skipping quietly.

L1.5  A MISSED ROUND IS NOT MADE UP LATER. See L2.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.  L2 - THE FAILURE RULE, DECIDED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

L2.1  THE DECISION. A matchweek whose predictions were not written before its
      first kickoff is CONTAMINATED. Those matches are

          EXCLUDED FROM THE LIVE LOG and from every figure the live log
          reports, with the gap recorded explicitly and counted;

          NOT EXCLUDED FROM THE HOLDOUT, which continues to contain every
          2026-27 match with date >= 2026-09-04, exactly as H3.3 and P1.4
          specify.

      NO LATE ROW IS WRITTEN.

L2.2  WHY THE SECOND HALF IS NOT OPTIONAL. The brief offered "excluded from
      the holdout with the gap reported" as one of two choices. THAT CHOICE
      WAS NOT AVAILABLE. The holdout's membership is a date rule in a frozen
      document; removing matches from it because an operator missed a
      deadline would make the holdout's size depend on operator diligence,
      which is the precise failure P1.3 already refused when it forbade the
      cutoff from tracking the commit timestamp. So "excluded" here means
      excluded from the LOG, and the holdout is untouched.

L2.3  WHY NO LATE ROW. The alternative was "generated late and flagged". A
      prediction computed after the result exists is not evidence of anything,
      whatever flag it carries, and its only effect is to put a row in an
      evidence file that a later reader may take at face value. The project
      has already recorded what that costs: V6b's misspelled side was scored
      as exactly average with no exception raised, and the corruption was
      indistinguishable from a result. A flag in a column is a weaker guard
      than a row that was never written.

L2.4  THE GAP IS A RECORD, NOT A SILENCE. A contaminated matchweek is written
      to live_log/phase6_live_contamination.csv with the round, the match
      count, the first kickoff, the time the omission was found, and the
      reason. The count is reported whenever the log is reported.

L2.5  APPLIES IMMEDIATELY, AND TO THREE MATCHWEEKS ALREADY. This protocol is
      declared on 2026-09-11. The holdout opened on 2026-09-04. The matchweek
      of 4-6 September 2026 - ten matches, the first being Ipswich Town v
      Liverpool, which P1.1 names as the holdout's first fixture - was played
      before this log existed. It is CONTAMINATED under L2.1 and is recorded
      as such. The live log therefore covers the holdout from its second
      matchweek onward, and says so rather than implying full coverage.

      The two matchweeks of August 2026 are before the cutoff. They are not
      in the holdout at all and are not a gap in anything.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.  L3 - THE POSTPONEMENT RULE, DECIDED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

L3.1  THE DECISION. A RESCHEDULED MATCH KEEPS ITS ORIGINAL PREDICTION AND ITS
      ORIGINAL STATE CUTOFF. It is never regenerated, and there is never a
      second row for the same match.

L3.2  WHY NOT THE OTHER OPTION. "Regenerated with a new cutoff and both rows
      retained" leaves two rows and no rule for which one counts. That choice
      would be made at analysis time, which is after the result is known -
      and a choice between two predictions made after seeing the outcome is
      selection on the outcome. It also contradicts L1.4 directly. One row,
      fixed at the moment it was written, is the only version of this log that
      means what it claims to mean.

L3.3  THE JOIN KEY IS THEREFORE NOT DATE-BASED. match_id is

          season + home_team + away_team

      which is unique because a twenty-team double round-robin plays each
      ORDERED pair exactly once, 20 x 19 = 380. Verified on all 1,900
      development matches: 1,900 rows, 1,900 distinct keys, 0 duplicates. A
      date-based key would break under the very rescheduling this clause
      exists to handle.

L3.4  THE COST IS REAL AND IS MEASURED PER ROW. A match played long after its
      matchweek carries a stale prediction. Every row records

          state_age_days  =  date actually played  -  state cutoff

      and it is populated at capture, not at generation, because at
      generation the date played is not yet known.

L3.5  THE STALE STRATUM IS PRE-DECLARED AT 10 DAYS. Rows with
      state_age_days > 10 are reported as a SEPARATE stratum. They are never
      dropped and never regenerated.

      The threshold is taken from the development distribution, before any
      2026-27 row existed: of 1,900 matches, 858 are played on their
      matchweek's first day, 1,743 within one day, 1,768 within two - and the
      distribution then stops at 10 days and resumes at 17. 101 matches
      (5.32%) are more than 10 days stale, the worst at 200 days. The
      threshold is placed in that empty gap rather than at a round number
      chosen for its roundness.

L3.6  A POSTPONEMENT DOES NOT MOVE THE HOLDOUT BOUNDARY EITHER. P1.4 already
      settles this: a fixture played on or after 2026-09-04 is scored because
      it was played after the cutoff, whatever date it was originally
      scheduled for. This clause changes nothing there.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.  L4 - IMMUTABILITY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

L4.1  APPEND-ONLY. Rows are added. No row is ever edited, reordered or
      deleted, in the mirror or in Supabase.

L4.2  row_hash IS A CHAIN, NOT A PER-ROW CHECKSUM.

          row_hash = sha256( prev_hash + "|" + the fields below )

      A bare per-row hash detects an EDITED row. It does not detect a DELETED
      row, or one inserted between two others - and an append-only log whose
      rows can be quietly removed is not append-only. Chaining makes every row
      depend on its entire history, so a deletion or a reordering breaks every
      hash after it.

      The first row chains to sixty-four zeroes.

L4.3  THE HASHED FIELDS, IN THIS ORDER, NEVER REORDERED:

          match_id, season, round_id, matchweek_label, scheduled_date,
          home_team, away_team, state_cutoff_date, generated_at_utc,
          p_home, p_draw, p_away, lambda_home, lambda_away, rho,
          fit_matches, home_has_history, away_has_history,
          freeze_sha, pin_sha, protocol_sha

      The list lives in ONE place, HASHED_FIELDS in the generator, and the
      verifier imports it rather than carrying a copy. Two copies of a field
      order is two things to keep in step, and the verifier would be the one
      that silently stopped checking.

L4.4  HASHED OVER PARSED FIELD VALUES, NEVER OVER FILE BYTES. This repository
      has already lost a hash to a line-ending conversion: on 2026-09-03 a
      fresh clone returned PHASE6_HOLDOUT_FREEZE.txt as 8373973b... against
      b36befd3... on disk, because core.autocrlf=true converted every LF on
      checkout. A row hash computed from parsed values cannot be moved by
      that. Floats are canonicalised at %.17g - the project's FLOAT_FORMAT -
      so the hash covers the full double and not a rounded display of it.

L4.5  THE VERIFIER RECOMPUTES EVERY HASH AND FAILS ON ANY MISMATCH.

          scripts/phase6_generate_predictions.py --verify

      It reports the row, the field, the stored value and the recomputation.
      It repairs nothing.

L4.6  THE VERIFIER IS ITSELF EXERCISED. The dry run builds a clean chain,
      checks that the verifier passes it, then moves a single written
      probability from 0.5 to 0.6 and checks that the verifier FAILS. A check
      that has never been seen to fire is not a check.

L4.7  THE LOG IS DELIBERATELY NOT IN FROZEN_MANIFEST.txt, AND THIS IS NOT AN
      OMISSION. FROZEN_PATTERNS contains the glob outputs/phase6_*.csv, so a
      Phase 6 CSV is swept into the manifest and pinned by hash. This log is
      append-only by design: its bytes change every round, so a pinned file
      hash would fail the manifest every matchweek and the failure would carry
      no information. It therefore lives in live_log/ instead, OUTSIDE that
      glob, and its immutability is enforced by the L4 chain and by RLS -
      which distinguish an APPEND from an EDIT, and a file hash cannot.

L4.8  WHAT IS COMMITTED, AND WHAT IS NOT.

          live_log/phase6_live_predictions.csv     COMMITTED
          live_log/phase6_live_contamination.csv   COMMITTED
          live_log/phase6_live_results.csv         NOT committed
          live_log/phase6_live_market.csv          NOT committed

      The predictions mirror carries no scoreline. Committing it is what
      timestamps it, and the timestamp is the whole point of the exercise.

      The results and market mirrors DO carry 2026-27 scorelines, and
      .gitignore already keeps those out of the repository until the holdout
      is deliberately acquired in May 2027 - the same rule that keeps
      data/watch/ out. That rule is not relaxed for this log.

5.  L5 - AUTHORITY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

L5.1  THE OFFICIAL 2026-27 RESULT IS phase6_score_holdout.py, run ONCE after
      the final fixture, against the pinned cutoff, behind both --score-holdout
      and --season-complete. That has not changed and this document does not
      change it.

L5.2  EVERY RUNNING FIGURE THE LOG OR THE DASHBOARD SHOWS IS INFORMAL. No
      mid-season figure enters REPORT.md, REPORTS.md, or any conclusion.

L5.3  AND THE LOG IS A DIFFERENT INSTRUMENT, NOT AN EARLY VIEW OF THE SAME
      ONE. This is stronger than L5.2 and it is the clause most likely to be
      forgotten in 2027, so the numbers are recorded here now.

      The frozen dc_walkforward refits ONCE PER DISTINCT SCORED DATE:

          2022-23  117 refits      2024-25  109 refits
          2023-24  120 refits      2025-26  114 refits

      A matchweek-cadence log refits 38 times, every season. 37 of 38
      matchweeks span more than one day, so most matches are predicted without
      that matchweek's earlier results. Measured on the development seasons,
      by the generator, before any 2026-27 row was written:

          season     rows differing   matchweek LL   per-date LL      cost
          2022-23        200 of 380      0.995942      0.997983   -0.002041
          2023-24        200 of 380      0.943645      0.941444   +0.002202
          2024-25        198 of 380      0.978967      0.976663   +0.002304
          2025-26        221 of 380      1.046553      1.045363   +0.001189

          pooled matchweek    0.9912769 LL     0.2037256 RPS
          pooled per-date     0.9903635 LL     0.2034988 RPS
          THE CADENCE COST   +0.0009134 LL    +0.0002268 RPS

L5.4  SO A LIVE-LOG FIGURE AND THE OFFICIAL FIGURE ARE NOT COMPARABLE, and
      the offset above is pre-declared so that it cannot be invented after the
      fact to explain a gap. The log's information set is STRICTLY SMALLER -
      38 fits against 109 to 120 - which is what licenses calling the
      difference a cost rather than a discrepancy.

L5.5  HONESTLY: THE COST IS SMALL AND IT IS NOT UNIFORM IN SIGN. Matchweek
      cadence was BETTER in 2022-23 and worse in the other three. Four seasons
      is four observations; the pooled +0.00091 is a measurement, not a law,
      and it is recorded as the former.

L5.6  0.99036 / 0.20350 IS THE POOLED FOUR-FOLD FIGURE OVER 1,520 MATCHES. It
      is not 2025-26's, which is 1.04536 / 0.21281. The brief for the
      generator asked that a 2025-26 matchweek-by-matchweek run reproduce
      0.99036 / 0.20350; it could not, for two independent reasons - the
      wrong season and the wrong cadence - and both are recorded here rather
      than absorbed. The scoring instrument records this project making the
      same conflation once before.

6.  L6 - NO FEEDBACK
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

L6.1  NOTHING OBSERVED IN THIS LOG MAY CHANGE ANYTHING. Not the model, not a
      feature, not a hyperparameter, not a rating, not an evaluation rule, not
      the cutoff, not the vocabulary, not the de-vig, not the reporting bins.

L6.2  ANY SUCH CHANGE MARKS THE HOLDOUT COMPROMISED, per the freeze, and the
      correct response is to record that it was compromised - not to repair it
      and carry on.

L6.3  THE ONE PERMITTED AMENDMENT is a dated amendment to THIS document, of
      the kind P4.5 already defines for the pin, and it may only change how
      the LOG operates. An amendment that changes the model is not an
      amendment; it is a new project on a spent holdout.

L6.4  THE VOCABULARY GUARD STILL RAISES, AND THAT IS STILL THE POINT. The
      generator asserts every name - in the fixtures it predicts and in the
      completed spine it fits on - through the scoring instrument's OWN
      assert_vocabulary. A name outside the pin's twenty stops the run. No
      normalisation was added to any loader for this work either, for the
      reason section 6 of the pin gives: a rule written to absorb one
      mismatch absorbs the next one silently.

7.  THE ONE NEW DEPENDENCY, DECLARED RATHER THAN DISCOVERED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

L7.1  A PRE-KICKOFF PREDICTION NEEDS TO KNOW THE FIXTURES, and no input the
      project already declares carries an UNPLAYED match. The E0 file carries
      completed matches only. So the log adds one source:

          https://www.football-data.co.uk/fixtures.csv

      filtered to Div == E0. Same publisher as the declared spine, mapped
      through the same closed TEAM_MAP, asserted against the same vocabulary.

L7.2  IT IS DECLARED HERE BECAUSE SW10 IS THE PRECEDENT. SW10 recorded that
      the scoring instrument's spine came from an FBref export that no watch
      covered, and closing that gap took a dated amendment. An input added
      without a declaration is the same defect. This one is declared on the
      day it is first used.

L7.3  IT CANNOT CONTAMINATE THE HOLDOUT. The fixture feed is read at
      generation time only, it carries no result, and nothing in the scoring
      path reads it. If it disappears, the log stops and the holdout is
      unaffected - which is the correct failure mode and is the reason it is
      acceptable for the log to depend on it at all.

L7.4  THE FEED ALSO CARRIES PRE-MATCH ODDS. They are NOT read by the
      generator. B3.3 fixes the market instrument on Bet365 CLOSING prices
      and the closing price does not exist until the match does; a pre-match
      price is a different quantity and mixing them would be a new
      instrument, not a convenience.

L8.2  WHY. A second copy of the Dixon-Coles fit would be a second thing to

      reimplementation, and DRY RUN A is the test of that claim: at the frozen
      per-date cadence it must reproduce every fold BIT FOR BIT. It does -
      worst absolute difference 0.000e+00 across all four folds and both
      published pooled figures.

L8.3  THE ESTIMATOR, THE VOCABULARY, THE CUTOFF AND THE DE-VIG ARE NOT
      REDEFINED ANYWHERE IN THIS WORK.

9.  SUPABASE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

L9.1  THREE TABLES: predictions, results, market. The schema is in
      ops/supabase_live_log.sql, versioned in the repository.

L9.2  RLS: INSERT permitted; UPDATE and DELETE have NO policy and are
      therefore denied to every role that goes through RLS. The anon key is
      SELECT-only and is what the frontend uses. Writes come from the local
      generator under the service key.

L9.3  THE SERVICE KEY IS NOT IN THE REPOSITORY. It is read from the
      environment, SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_SERVICE_KEY, in one function.

L9.4  THE LOCAL MIRROR IS THE PRIMARY RECORD, not Supabase. Git is what
      timestamps a row in a way the operator cannot backdate. A run with no
      credentials writes the mirror, reports that Supabase was not written,
      and is a DEGRADED run rather than a failed one.

L9.5  SERVICE-ROLE WRITES BYPASS RLS, so L4's immutability rests on the
      generator's own refusal to rewrite an existing match_id, plus the chain,
      plus the mirror in git. RLS protects the log from the frontend and from
      anyone holding the anon key. It does not protect it from the service
      key, and this clause says so rather than implying otherwise.

1.  THE CUTOFF DATE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

P1.1  THE PINNED CUTOFF IS

          2026-09-04     (Friday 4 September 2026)

      First fixture on or after it: IPSWICH TOWN v LIVERPOOL.

P1.2  HOW H3.1's RULE WAS EVALUATED. H3.1 defines the cutoff as the first
      2026-27 fixture date on or after the date the freeze is committed. The
      rule was evaluated on 2026-09-03. The first 2026-27 fixture date on or
      after 2026-09-03 is 2026-09-04.

P1.3  THE DATE IS ABSOLUTE FROM THAT EVALUATION. THE RULE SELECTED IT; THE
      RULE DOES NOT RE-FIRE.

      Stated explicitly because the alternative is worse. If the cutoff
      tracked the commit timestamp, then a commit delayed to 5 September
      would silently move Ipswich Town v Liverpool from the holdout into
      warming history - and the holdout's size would depend on when someone
      got round to typing `git commit`. A holdout whose boundary moves with
      operator convenience is not a holdout.

      So: 2026-09-04, regardless of when this is committed, and any later
      commit does NOT re-evaluate H3.1.

P1.4  THE RULE IS DATE-BASED, NOT MATCHWEEK-LABEL-BASED, per H3.4.

          date >= 2026-09-04    SCORED (holdout)
          date <  2026-09-04    WARMS THE STATE, not scored

      A fixture postponed from before the cutoff to after it is SCORED,
      because it is played after the cutoff. A fixture postponed from after
      the cutoff to before it - which requires bringing a match forward -
      would be warming history. The direction of the rule does not change
      with the direction of the postponement.

P1.5  THE STRICT WINDOW RULE IS UNAFFECTED. H2.5's `date < cutoff` continues
      to govern which matches enter each REFIT WINDOW. A match played on
      2026-09-04 is scored, and is not in the window that predicts it. The
      two rules are different things and both hold.

4.  THE 2026-27 VOCABULARY.  TWENTY NAMES, DECLARED.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

P4.1  WHY THIS IS IN A FROZEN DOCUMENT AND NOT IN CODE. V6b of the freeze
      validator demonstrated the failure it prevents, on the live
      match_rates():

          "Manchester Utd"  ->  home rate 1.1186
          "Man United"      ->  home rate 0.8165        NO EXCEPTION RAISED

      A misspelled KNOWN team does not error. It falls through
      .fillna(NEUTRAL_STRENGTH) and is silently scored as an exactly average
      side. Over a season that would corrupt the holdout invisibly and the
      corruption would be indistinguishable from a genuine result.

P4.2  THE SEVENTEEN CONTINUING SIDES, SPELLED AS THE FROZEN DATASET SPELLS
      THEM. These are verified against outputs/phase1_matches.csv and are
      the names the estimator will actually hold:

          Arsenal              Aston Villa          Bournemouth
          Brentford            Brighton             Chelsea
          Crystal Palace       Everton              Fulham
          Leeds United         Liverpool            Manchester City
          Manchester Utd       Newcastle            Nottingham
          Sunderland           Tottenham

      The dataset's vocabulary is its own and is NOT tidied: "Manchester
      Utd" not "Manchester United"; "Nottingham" not "Nottingham Forest";
      "Newcastle" not "Newcastle United"; "Brighton", "Bournemouth",
      "Tottenham", "Wolves" all short. H2.12 makes these specification.

P4.3  THE THREE PROMOTED SIDES.   AMENDED 2026-09-03 - SEE SECTION 6.

          Ipswich Town     present in the dataset, 2024-25 only
          Coventry         NO history in the five-season dataset
          Hull             NO history in the five-season dataset

      "Ipswich Town" is the dataset's own spelling and needs no ruling.
      "Coventry" and "Hull" ARE THE SOURCE FILE'S OWN SPELLINGS, adopted by
      amendment on 2026-09-03 after the source-integrity watch read them off
      the 2026-27 file. They were written here originally as "Coventry City"
      and "Hull City", taken from the published fixture list, because the
      dataset had no precedent to defer to. It has one now - the file the
      scoring run will actually read. Section 6 records the change, its date,
      and why the repair was made HERE and not inside a loader.

P4.4  THE TWENTY-NAME VOCABULARY IS THEREFORE P4.2 + P4.3. West Ham,
      Burnley and Wolves were relegated from 2025-26 and are NOT in it,
      though they remain in the historical dataset and continue to
      contribute to warming windows through the decay.

P4.5  THE ASSERTION THE SCORING RUN MUST MAKE. Every team name appearing in
      2026-27 data must be in the twenty above. A name that is not must
      RAISE AND STOP THE RUN. It must not be repaired, mapped, normalised or
      guessed at.

      IF THE 2026-27 SOURCE SPELLS COVENTRY OR HULL DIFFERENTLY - "Coventry"
      or "Hull", say - THE RUN FAILS, AND THAT IS THE CORRECT BEHAVIOUR. The
      fix is a dated amendment to this file recording the source's actual
      spelling, made deliberately, and NOT a silent normalisation inside a
      loader. A loader that repairs names is a loader that can repair the
      wrong one.

      THIS HAPPENED ON 2026-09-03. See section 6.

P4.6  THE ZERO-HISTORY SET, for H6.6's split.   AMENDED 2026-09-03:

          Coventry, Hull

      Ipswich Town is NOT in it - it has 2024-25, which is thin rather than
      absent. The 107-day half-life renders one old season nearly weightless
      by September 2026, but "nearly weightless" and "absent" are different
      claims and only the second one belongs in a set called zero-history.

6.  AMENDMENT 1, DATED 2026-09-03 - THE SOURCE'S SPELLINGS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

P6.1  WHAT CHANGED.

                 "Hull City"      ->  "Hull"
          P4.6   the zero-history set follows P4.3

      The vocabulary is still twenty names. The zero-history set is still the

P6.2  HOW IT WAS FOUND, AND ON WHAT DATE.

      scripts/phase6_source_watch.py, first run, 2026-09-03. Check SW6 read
      the team names off the 2026-27 football-data.co.uk file, applied the
      project's own TEAM_MAP whole, and compared the result to the twenty
      names of section 4:

          2 outside the vocabulary:  Coventry, Hull

      Recorded in outputs/phase6_source_watch_2026-09-03.csv. The run that
      found it is committed at d7bde51, BEFORE this amendment, so the finding
      and the fix are separate objects in the history rather than one.

P6.3  THE GUARD DISCRIMINATED. IT DID NOT SIMPLY FIRE.

      A guard that always fires is not a guard, so the check was run as a
      pair on the same frame:

          against the pin as it stood        SW6 FAIL, exactly 2 names
          against the source's own spellings SW6 PASS, 0 names

      Either half alone is consistent with a guard that never fires or one
      that always does. The pair is the test, and it is the same construction
      the scoring instrument's own S5a/S5/S6 use.

P6.4  NO NORMALISATION WAS ADDED TO ANY LOADER, AND THIS IS THE POINT OF THE
      AMENDMENT RATHER THAN A DETAIL OF IT.

      TEAM_MAP is unchanged. Neither "Coventry" nor "Hull" was mapped,
      aliased, normalised, title-cased or fuzzy-matched anywhere in the code.
      The repair is in this document, which is data the run reads, and it is
      dated.

      THE REASON. A loader that repairs a name repairs the NEXT one too, and
      the next one may be the failure V6b demonstrated on the live
      match_rates():

          "Manchester Utd"  ->  home rate 1.1186
          "Man United"      ->  home rate 0.8165        NO EXCEPTION RAISED

      A misspelled KNOWN team does not error. It falls through to
      NEUTRAL_STRENGTH and is scored as an exactly average side, and over a
      season that corrupts the holdout invisibly. A normalisation rule
      written today to absorb "Coventry" is a rule that would absorb
      "Man United" in April, silently, and nothing would say so.

      SO THE GUARD MUST KEEP RAISING. Every future mismatch must stop the run
      and be resolved by another dated amendment here, deliberately, with the
      source's actual spelling written down. That is slower. It is the only
      version of this that stays checkable.

P6.5  WHY THIS IS CONSISTENT WITH H2.12 RATHER THAN AN EXCEPTION TO IT.

      H2.12 requires that every 2026-27 name either MATCHES A DATASET NAME
      EXACTLY or is ONE OF THE THREE DECLARED PROMOTED SIDES. The seventeen
      continuing sides keep the dataset's own spellings and are untouched -
      "Manchester Utd", "Nottingham", "Newcastle", "Leeds United". Coventry
      and Hull appear in no dataset season under any spelling, so there is no
      dataset name for them to match and the freeze leaves their spelling to
      this document. The amendment operates exactly where the freeze left the
      choice open, and nowhere else.

      IPSWICH NEEDS NO AMENDMENT, and the asymmetry is deliberate rather than
      an oversight. The source spells it "Ipswich"; TEAM_MAP - the closed
      dictionary declared at B2.2 of PHASE5_MARKET_PREDECLARATION.txt, in
      place since Phase 5B and asserted closed at M3 - maps that to "Ipswich
      Town", which IS the dataset's own spelling, because Ipswich has a
      2024-25 season in the data. A club with history defers to the dataset;
      a club with none defers to the source. That is the rule, stated so the
      two cases are not read as an inconsistency.

P6.6  THIS DOES NOT TRIGGER H5.3, AND HERE IS THE ARGUMENT RATHER THAN THE
      ASSERTION.

      H5.3 covers a change forced by a defect and made AFTER OBSERVING
      2026-27 DATA. H5.4 covers the opposite case in terms: "fixing a defect
      that would prevent the declared model from running at all - a crash, a
      join that drops rows, A NAME THAT FAILS TO MATCH UNDER H2.12. Repairing
      the implementation so that it does what this file already says is not
      amending what this file says."

      This is that case, named in the freeze in advance.

      AND NO 2026-27 RESULT HAS BEEN OBSERVED. The watch loads four columns -
      Div, Date, HomeTeam, AwayTeam - and SW2b asserts that no result column
      reaches memory. What was read is a team name, which is schedule
      information available from any fixture list and carries no outcome.

      The date of the repair is logged here because H5.4 requires it, and the
      distinction is argued rather than assumed.

7.  AMENDMENT 2, DATED 2026-09-03 - THE DECLARED SPINE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

P7.1  THE GAP THIS CLOSES. SW10 of the source watch recorded that the scoring
      instrument reads outputs/phase1_matches.csv, which is built from an
      FBref fixture export in data/raw/Fixtures/ - not from the file the
      watch checks. That export arrives only at season end, so it was the one
      input to the holdout with NO ADVANCE CHECK OF ANY KIND.

P7.2  THE DECLARATION. THE 2026-27 SPINE IS THE FOOTBALL-DATA.CO.UK E0 FILE.

          date        <-  Date        (%d/%m/%Y)
          home_team   <-  HomeTeam    through TEAM_MAP
          away_team   <-  AwayTeam    through TEAM_MAP
          home_goals  <-  FTHG
          away_goals  <-  FTAG
          result      <-  FTR

      matchweek is NOT required and is not carried. H3.4 makes the cutoff a
      DATE rule explicitly - "the rule is about dates played, not about
      matchweek labels" - and no other part of the scoring path reads it.

P7.3  THE SUBSTITUTION IS CLEAN, AND IT IS DEMONSTRATED RATHER THAN ARGUED.
      The two sources have already been reconciled on all 1,900 development
      matches, by an instrument committed before this question was asked
      (outputs/phase5_market_audit.csv):

          M1b   every one of the project's 1,900 matches joins exactly one
                odds row.  1900 matched, 0 unmatched
          M2a   football-data.co.uk and the project's own FBref-derived
                foundation agree on EVERY SCORELINE.  0 disagreements
          M2b   and on the DATE of every match.  0 disagreements
          M3    the team mapping is closed - 27 names a side, 0 unmapped

      And each of the five E0 files carries exactly 380 rows, so the
      correspondence is one-to-one and not merely a successful join.

      Two independently sourced spines that agree on every date and every
      scoreline over five seasons are the same spine. The substitution
      changes no value the model would see.

P7.4  THE SCORING INSTRUMENT'S BEHAVIOUR IS NOT CHANGED BY THIS AMENDMENT.
      It still reads outputs/phase1_matches.csv and still asserts H2.12,
      H3.3, H6.6 and H7 exactly as before. What this section declares is the
      PROVENANCE of the 2026-27 rows in that file: they are built from the E0
      file at season end, in the schema phase1_match_foundation.py already
      produces, and not from an FBref export.

P7.5  TEAM_MAP IS NOT A REPAIR AND ITS USE HERE IS NOT A NORMALISATION.
      It is a closed, explicit dictionary declared at B2.2 before any score
      existed, applied by whole-name lookup and never by partial match, and
      asserted closed at M3. A name absent from it passes through UNCHANGED
      so that the vocabulary assertion of section 4 can see it and fail on
      it - which is exactly what happened to Coventry and Hull, and exactly
      what section 6 says must keep happening.

P7.6  WHAT IS LOST, STATED BECAUSE IT IS A REAL COST. On the five development
      seasons, TWO INDEPENDENT SOURCES agreed on every scoreline and every
      date, and that agreement is itself evidence about the data. A
      single-source spine has no such cross-check.

      THE MITIGATION IS NOT TO KEEP BOTH AS SPINES. If the FBref 2026-27
      export exists at season end it should be reconciled against the E0
      spine as a CHECK, reported like M2a and M2b, and never used as the
      spine - because a run that can silently pick either source is a run
      whose vocabulary and row count depend on which one it picked.

P7.7  NOT A FREEZE AMENDMENT. PHASE6_HOLDOUT_FREEZE.txt names the model, its
      constants, the cutoff rule and what is scored. IT NAMES NO SPINE FILE.
      Declaring the input is what this document is for. Dated and reasoned
      here anyway, because an input declaration made after the season starts
      is exactly the kind of thing that should be checkable.

P7.8  THE CONSEQUENCE. Every input the scoring run has is now the file the
      watch checks monthly. SW10 is no longer a gap in the watch's cover, and
      the watch is amended to assert this declaration rather than to report
      the FBref dependency it replaces.

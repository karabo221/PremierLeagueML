P0.3  THE REASON. Writing the date into PHASE6_HOLDOUT_FREEZE.txt would move

P0.5  H7's slot in the freeze file therefore stays as printed:

1.  THE CUTOFF DATE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

P1.1  THE PINNED CUTOFF IS

          2026-09-04     (Friday 4 September 2026)

      First fixture on or after it: IPSWICH TOWN v LIVERPOOL.

P1.2  HOW H3.1's RULE WAS EVALUATED. H3.1 defines the cutoff as the first
      2026-27 fixture date on or after the date the freeze is committed. The
      rule was evaluated on 2026-09-03. The first 2026-27 fixture date on or
      after 2026-09-03 is 2026-09-04.

P1.3  THE DATE IS ABSOLUTE FROM THAT EVALUATION. THE RULE SELECTED IT; THE
      RULE DOES NOT RE-FIRE.

      Stated explicitly because the alternative is worse. If the cutoff
      tracked the commit timestamp, then a commit delayed to 5 September
      would silently move Ipswich Town v Liverpool from the holdout into
      warming history - and the holdout's size would depend on when someone
      got round to typing `git commit`. A holdout whose boundary moves with
      operator convenience is not a holdout.

      So: 2026-09-04, regardless of when this is committed, and any later
      commit does NOT re-evaluate H3.1.

P1.4  THE RULE IS DATE-BASED, NOT MATCHWEEK-LABEL-BASED, per H3.4.

          date >= 2026-09-04    SCORED (holdout)
          date <  2026-09-04    WARMS THE STATE, not scored

      A fixture postponed from before the cutoff to after it is SCORED,
      because it is played after the cutoff. A fixture postponed from after
      the cutoff to before it - which requires bringing a match forward -
      would be warming history. The direction of the rule does not change
      with the direction of the postponement.

P1.5  THE STRICT WINDOW RULE IS UNAFFECTED. H2.5's `date < cutoff` continues
      to govern which matches enter each REFIT WINDOW. A match played on
      2026-09-04 is scored, and is not in the window that predicts it. The
      two rules are different things and both hold.

2.  H3.2's EXPECTATION WAS WRONG, AND HERE IS WHY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

P2.1  H3.2 EXPECTED "around 12 September 2026", reasoning that matchweeks 1
      and 2 were complete and an international break followed. THE BREAK
      REASONING WAS WRONG.

P2.2  THE 2026-27 CALENDAR REDUCES FIVE INTERNATIONAL BREAKS TO FOUR, with a
      three-week break between LATE SEPTEMBER AND EARLY OCTOBER - the first
      time such a block has been used. THERE IS NO EARLY-SEPTEMBER BREAK
      THIS SEASON, so nothing pushes matchweek 3 back and it follows
      matchweek 2 at the normal weekly interval.

          matchweek 1     21-24 August 2026
          matchweek 2     28-31 August 2026
          matchweek 3      4-6  September 2026

P2.3  H3.2 ANTICIPATED BEING WRONG AND SAID WHICH WAY TO RESOLVE IT: "IF THE
      ACTUAL FIXTURE LIST DISAGREES, THE RULE WINS, NOT THIS EXPECTATION."
      The rule won. The expectation is recorded here as wrong rather than
      quietly dropped, because a pre-declaration whose failed guesses are
      deleted is not a pre-declaration.

P2.4  THE ERROR COST NOTHING. The expectation was never load-bearing - H3.1
      is a rule over a fixture list, not over a guess. Had the expectation
      been used as the date, the holdout would have LOST the ten matchweek-3
      matches it is now entitled to.

4.  THE 2026-27 VOCABULARY.  TWENTY NAMES, DECLARED.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

P4.1  WHY THIS IS IN A FROZEN DOCUMENT AND NOT IN CODE. V6b of the freeze
      validator demonstrated the failure it prevents, on the live
      match_rates():

          "Manchester Utd"  ->  home rate 1.1186
          "Man United"      ->  home rate 0.8165        NO EXCEPTION RAISED

      A misspelled KNOWN team does not error. It falls through
      .fillna(NEUTRAL_STRENGTH) and is silently scored as an exactly average
      side. Over a season that would corrupt the holdout invisibly and the
      corruption would be indistinguishable from a genuine result.

P4.2  THE SEVENTEEN CONTINUING SIDES, SPELLED AS THE FROZEN DATASET SPELLS
      THEM. These are verified against outputs/phase1_matches.csv and are
      the names the estimator will actually hold:

          Arsenal              Aston Villa          Bournemouth
          Brentford            Brighton             Chelsea
          Crystal Palace       Everton              Fulham
          Leeds United         Liverpool            Manchester City
          Manchester Utd       Newcastle            Nottingham
          Sunderland           Tottenham

      The dataset's vocabulary is its own and is NOT tidied: "Manchester
      Utd" not "Manchester United"; "Nottingham" not "Nottingham Forest";
      "Newcastle" not "Newcastle United"; "Brighton", "Bournemouth",
      "Tottenham", "Wolves" all short. H2.12 makes these specification.

P4.3  THE THREE PROMOTED SIDES:

          Ipswich Town     present in the dataset, 2024-25 only
          Coventry City    NO history in the five-season dataset
          Hull City        NO history in the five-season dataset

      "Ipswich Town" is the dataset's own spelling and needs no ruling.
      "Coventry City" and "Hull City" are spelled as the published fixture
      list spells them, because the dataset has no precedent to defer to.

P4.4  THE TWENTY-NAME VOCABULARY IS THEREFORE P4.2 + P4.3. West Ham,
      Burnley and Wolves were relegated from 2025-26 and are NOT in it,
      though they remain in the historical dataset and continue to
      contribute to warming windows through the decay.

P4.5  THE ASSERTION THE SCORING RUN MUST MAKE. Every team name appearing in
      2026-27 data must be in the twenty above. A name that is not must
      RAISE AND STOP THE RUN. It must not be repaired, mapped, normalised or
      guessed at.

      IF THE 2026-27 SOURCE SPELLS COVENTRY OR HULL DIFFERENTLY - "Coventry"
      or "Hull", say - THE RUN FAILS, AND THAT IS THE CORRECT BEHAVIOUR. The
      fix is a dated amendment to this file recording the source's actual
      spelling, made deliberately, and NOT a silent normalisation inside a
      loader. A loader that repairs names is a loader that can repair the
      wrong one.

P4.6  THE ZERO-HISTORY SET, for H6.6's split:

          Coventry City, Hull City

      Ipswich Town is NOT in it - it has 2024-25, which is thin rather than
      absent. The 107-day half-life renders one old season nearly weightless
      by September 2026, but "nearly weightless" and "absent" are different
      claims and only the second one belongs in a set called zero-history.

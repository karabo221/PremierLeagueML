PHASE 5 - INSTRUMENT B:  THE MARKET BENCHMARK AND THE CALIBRATION AUDIT
PRE-DECLARATION, WRITTEN BEFORE ANY SCORE WAS COMPUTED
===============================================================================

STATUS      Declared 2026-09-02, before the instrument was written and before
            any market log loss, RPS or calibration figure existed.

            Everything in sections 1 to 6 was decided from PROPERTIES OF THE
            SOURCE FILES - completeness, panel composition, join integrity -
            and not from any score. Section 7 states what would have made
            those decisions contaminated, so a later reader can check.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0.  THE EXCLUSION THIS LIFTS, AND ON WHOSE AUTHORITY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

B0.1  Step 2 of the Phase 4 brief excluded market odds, alongside xG,
      XGBoost, random forests and betting backtests. That exclusion has
      been LIFTED for odds by explicit direction, as Phase 5 item B.

B0.2  WHAT IS LIFTED IS NARROW. Odds enter as a BENCHMARK TO BE SCORED
      AGAINST, never as a feature. No rung of the D-ladder gains an odds
      column, nothing is re-fitted, and no number already reported moves.

B0.3  THE BETTING-BACKTEST EXCLUSION STANDS. This instrument computes no
      stake, no return, no yield and no closing-line value. It measures
      where the project sits against the market on the project's own
      metrics. XGBoost and random forests stay excluded. xG is Phase 5
      item A and is not in scope here.

2.  THE JOIN, AND THE TEAM MAPPING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

B2.1  JOIN KEY: (season, home_team, away_team). Not date. Two sources can
      disagree about the date of a rearranged fixture while agreeing about
      the fixture; the key that cannot drift is who played whom in which
      season. The date is then RECONCILED rather than joined on (M2).

B2.2  THE MAPPING IS EXPLICIT AND CLOSED. Eight names differ between the
      sources. They are listed here in full, and the instrument fails if
      any name on either side is not accounted for. NOTHING IS
      FUZZY-MATCHED - Phase 0 Instrument 4's rule, unchanged:

          Ipswich          -> Ipswich Town
          Leeds            -> Leeds United
          Leicester        -> Leicester City
          Luton            -> Luton Town
          Man City         -> Manchester City
          Man United       -> Manchester Utd
          Norwich          -> Norwich City
          Nott'm Forest    -> Nottingham

      Twenty-seven teams appear on each side. A twenty-eighth name from
      either source is a FAILURE, not a row to drop.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.  WHICH ODDS, AND WHY - DECIDED FROM COMPLETENESS, NOT FROM SCORE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

B3.1  THE PROBLEM THAT DRIVES THIS SECTION. The obvious benchmark is
      Pinnacle closing (PSCH/PSCD/PSCA), the sharpest line and the usual
      choice in the literature. It cannot be the primary here:

          PSC is missing on 170 of 1,900 matches, and ALL 170 ARE IN
          2025-2026 - fold 4's test season, where it covers 210 of 380.

      A primary benchmark absent from 45% of the most recent test fold
      would be a benchmark that silently changes population by fold.

B3.2  THE PANEL BEHIND "MARKET AVERAGE" IS NOT CONSTANT EITHER. Counting
      bookmakers with a closing home price present, by season:

          2021-2022   B365 BW PS WH VC IW              (IW 377)
          2022-2023   B365 BW PS WH VC IW
          2023-2024   B365 BW PS WH VC IW              (BW 368, IW 198)
          2024-2025   B365 BW BF PS WH 1XB BFE         (BW 239, WH 289)
          2025-2026   B365 BW PS LB BFE                (PS 210, LB 281)

      AvgC is complete on all 1,900 rows, but it is an average over a
      DIFFERENT SET OF BOOKMAKERS in each season. Its overround moves with
      the panel: 1.0388 in 2021-22 rising to 1.0567 in 2025-26, the last
      of which is an average taken largely without Pinnacle.

B3.3  THE DECLARED PRIMARY IS BET365 CLOSING (B365CH/B365CD/B365CA).

      It is the only bookmaker present and COMPLETE - 380 of 380 - in all
      five seasons. It is therefore the only column that is THE SAME
      INSTRUMENT AT EVERY FOLD, which is the property a cross-fold
      benchmark needs and the property neither PSC nor AvgC has.

      This is not a claim that Bet365 is the sharpest line. It is a claim
      that it is the only one that does not change what it is between
      fold 1 and fold 4.

B3.4  TWO SENSITIVITIES, DECLARED NOW, REPORTED WHATEVER THEY SAY.

      (a) AvgC, all 1,900 rows, with B3.2's panel caveat attached to every
          figure it produces.

      (b) PSC, RESTRICTED TO THE 1,730 ROWS WHERE IT EXISTS, reported PER
          FOLD and NEVER POOLED against the other two. A pooled Pinnacle
          number over a different row set than the primary is the kind of
          comparison that looks like evidence and is not.

      If the primary and the sensitivities disagree about where the
      project sits, that disagreement is the result and is reported as
      one. The primary is not swapped for whichever reads better.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.  FROM PRICES TO PROBABILITIES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

B4.1  DECLARED PRIMARY: PROPORTIONAL NORMALISATION.

          p_i  =  (1 / o_i)  /  SUM_j (1 / o_j)

      The simplest de-vig, the one most reported results use, and the one
      that assumes the bookmaker's margin is spread proportionally across
      the three outcomes.

B4.2  DECLARED SENSITIVITY: SHIN (1992), solved for z by bisection on the
      same three prices. Shin assumes part of the margin is protection
      against informed money and redistributes it away from longshots. It
      is the standard alternative and it moves draw and away prices in
      opposite directions from B4.1.

      BOTH ARE REPORTED. The primary is B4.1 and does not change.

B4.3  NO OTHER TRANSFORMATION. No shrinkage toward the base rate, no
      blending of bookmakers, no per-season recalibration of the market.
      The market is scored as it stands, because a market that has been
      fitted to this data is no longer an external benchmark.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.  WHAT IS SCORED, AND OVER WHICH ROWS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

B5.1  THE SAME 1,520 OUTER-TEST MATCHES, the same four frozen folds, and
      the full Phase 0 metric set through the harness's own evaluate().
      Every market probability array passes validate_probabilities()
      before it is scored, exactly as every model's does.

B5.2  THE COMPARISON SET is D0, D1, D2 rescaled, D3, D4, Elo v1, Poisson
      and Dixon-Coles, read from the committed artefacts. NOTHING IS
      RE-FITTED BY THIS INSTRUMENT.

B5.3  DELTAS use the project's paired per-match bootstrap, 10,000 draws,
      seed 20260901, and the same binding verdict rule: RPS must agree in
      sign with log loss or the comparison is INCONCLUSIVE.

      Declared pairs: market - D4, market - D2rescaled, market - Elo v1,
      market - Dixon-Coles, market - D0. Pooled and per fold.

B5.4  THE HEADLINE NUMBER THIS REPLACES. Phase 4 recorded "the market's
      ~0.95 is out of reach by construction". That 0.95 is an ASSUMPTION
      carried in prose and has never been measured on these matches. This
      instrument replaces it with a measurement, and the measurement
      stands whether it is above or below 0.95.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.  THE CALIBRATION AUDIT, DECLARED BEFORE IT IS RUN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

B6.1  The project has tracked calibration and never made it a first-class
      result. It becomes one here, on the same 1,520 matches, for the
      market and for every model in B5.2.

B6.2  BINS ARE DECLARED NOW, NOT CHOSEN AFTER SEEING THE CURVE: ten fixed
      bins of width 0.1 on [0, 1], applied to each class separately and
      pooled over the three classes. A bin with fewer than 20 predictions
      is reported with its count and is NOT smoothed, merged or dropped.

B6.3  REPORTED PER MODEL: expected calibration error (ECE) weighted by bin
      count, maximum calibration error (MCE), the full reliability table
      (bin, n, mean predicted, observed frequency) per class, and the
      mean predicted minus observed frequency per class as a bias figure.

B6.4  ECE IS A DESCRIPTION, NOT A RANKING. A model may be better
      calibrated and worse on log loss; log loss and RPS remain the
      metrics that decide anything in this project. No model is declared
      better on ECE.

B6.5  NO RECALIBRATION IS FITTED. Not Platt, not isotonic, not
      temperature. Fitting a correction on the outer-test rows would be
      scoring on data used to fit, which is the one thing every gate in
      this project exists to prevent. If the audit shows a fixable bias,
      that is a Phase 5 finding and its fix is a separate, declared piece
      of work.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.  GATES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

      M1   every one of the 1,900 project matches joins exactly one odds
           row, and no odds row is used twice
      M2   the two sources agree on FTHG/FTAG and on the date for every
           joined match; any disagreement is reported per match and FAILS
      M3   the team mapping is closed - 27 names on each side, no
           unmapped name, no fuzzy match
      M4   the primary odds column is complete on all 1,520 scored rows
      M5   every de-vigged probability array passes the harness's
           validate_probabilities()
      M6   proportional de-vig returns rows summing to 1 within 1e-12, and
           Shin's solved z lies in [0, 1) with its bisection converged
      M7   the market's per-fold row counts are exactly 380, 380, 380, 380
      M8   pooled log loss equals the unweighted mean of the four fold
           values, to 1e-12 - the identity that has caught two bugs
      M9   the model figures this instrument prints match the committed
           artefacts bit for bit; nothing is re-derived
      M10  reliability bin counts sum to the number of predictions, per
           class and pooled

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.  WHAT WOULD HAVE MADE THIS CONTAMINATED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

      Choosing the bookmaker after seeing which gave the market its worst
      score. Choosing the de-vig after seeing which narrowed the gap.
      Moving the calibration bins after seeing the reliability curve.
      Dropping the 170 Pinnacle-less matches from the PRIMARY rather than
      choosing a primary that does not need them. Reporting ECE as a win.

      Section 3's choice is the one most exposed to this, which is why the
      completeness table is written out in full above: the reasoning is
      checkable against the source files without running anything.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.  WHAT THIS INSTRUMENT CANNOT ESTABLISH
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

      It cannot establish that the market is a ceiling. It measures where
      the market sits on these 1,520 matches; whether that is reachable
      without lineup and injury data is not a question these numbers
      answer.

      It cannot make the project's numbers out-of-sample. 2025-2026 was
      scored during Phase 3's lambda sweep and its B0-B6 ablation. The
      market has never been fitted to anything, so the comparison is
      between a development estimate and a clean external benchmark, and
      it is BIASED IN THE PROJECT'S FAVOUR. That direction is stated
      before the numbers, and it does not change after them.

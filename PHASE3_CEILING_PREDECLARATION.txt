===============================================================================
PHASE 3 - INSTRUMENT 5
CEILING RESOLUTION, AND THE FROZEN REGULARISATION CHOICE

PRE-DECLARATION.  Written before the experiment was run.
===============================================================================

WHY THIS EXPERIMENT EXISTS

    Instrument 4 ran the declared grid 0.01 ... 100 and every one of its 28
    (rung, fold) selections landed on the ceiling, lambda = 100. Nothing hit
    the floor. Its own pre-declaration section 3 forbade extending the grid
    mid-experiment and required exactly this: a second experiment, with its
    own pre-declaration.

    The ceiling hit is not cosmetic. As lambda goes to infinity every rung's
    fit collapses onto the intercept, which IS B0, whose mean log loss is
    1.068888. At lambda = 100 B1 scores 1.007648 - BELOW that limit. A curve
    that is below its own asymptote at the edge of the grid must turn back up
    somewhere beyond it. So Instrument 4 did not find B1's optimum; it found
    the edge of the box it was allowed to look in.

    This instrument opens the box in the one direction the evidence points,
    and then stops opening it: the outcome is a FROZEN choice, so that the
    penalty stops being a live parameter for the rest of the project.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  THE GRID
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    LAMBDA_GRID = 100, 300, 1000, 3000, 10000

    Five values, half-decade spacing, two further decades of range.

    100 is deliberately shared with Instrument 4's grid. It is the anchor: the
    two experiments must agree exactly at that point, and test C1 asserts it
    to < 1e-9 rather than trusting it. That chains the reproduction all the
    way down - Instrument 5 at lambda = 100 equals Instrument 4 at lambda =
    100, and Instrument 4 at lambda = 1 equals Instrument 3 at lambda = 1.

    The COMBINED grid across both instruments is therefore

        0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, 10, 30, 100, 300, 1000, 3000, 10000

    thirteen points over six decades, and the combined curve is reported.

    This grid is final. If a selection lands on 10000 the run reports it and
    the freeze is BLOCKED (section 4). It does not extend the grid again.
    Two extensions is a search.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.  THE PROTOCOL IS INSTRUMENT 4'S, UNCHANGED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Not re-specified here, because re-specifying it is how two things that are
    supposed to be identical drift apart. Instrument 5 IMPORTS Instrument 4's
    Run class, its fitted pipeline, its inner-split construction, its audit
    battery and its report functions, and passes a different grid.

    Restated only for the record:

        model         multinomial logistic, softmax over [H, D, A], L2,
                      intercept unpenalised, Newton to 1e-9
        selection     expanding-window inner CV over CALENDAR-DATE blocks
                      (Instrument 4, Amendment 1), cut from the outer fold's
                      TRAINING rows alone, four segments, three inner folds
        criterion     argmin of mean inner-validation log loss
        tie-break     exact ties go to the LARGER lambda
        after         refit on the full outer training rows at the selected
                      lambda, score the outer test season once
        oracle        the full outer-test surface is computed and reported,
                      labelled ORACLE, and read by nothing

    ONE CODE CHANGE IS REQUIRED AND IS DECLARED HERE

        Instrument 4 reads its grid from a module-level constant. To let
        Instrument 5 supply a different one, Run.__init__ and the report
        helpers gain an optional `grid` argument DEFAULTING to that same
        constant. This is behaviour-preserving: with the argument omitted,
        Instrument 4 runs exactly as it did.

        This is a refactor of a script whose results are already on disk, so
        it is not taken on trust. C1 re-derives Instrument 4's lambda = 100
        column from scratch through the refactored code and requires it to
        match the stored artefact to < 1e-9, on all six metrics, for all 28
        rung x fold pairs. If the refactor changed anything, C1 fails.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.  WHAT THIS INSTRUMENT IS TRYING TO FIND OUT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Q1  Where is the interior optimum? Every rung's outer-test curve must
        turn around before it reaches B0's 1.068888. Does it, inside this
        grid?

    Q2  Does the rung ordering survive? Instrument 4 found B1 best at all
        nine of its lambdas. B2-B6's deficit against B1 shrank monotonically
        as lambda rose - from 0.2559 at lambda = 0.01 to 0.0215 at lambda =
        100 - which is what convergence to a common limit looks like, but
        also what an impending crossover would look like. Two more decades
        settles it.

    Q3  Does B1 still beat the base rate once the penalty is right, and by
        how much? At lambda = 1 the margin was 0.0020. At lambda = 100 it was
        0.0613. This is the quantity that says whether Phase 1's results-only
        features carry anything at all.

    Q1, Q2 and Q3 are all answered from the SELECTED-lambda table and from the
    oracle surface, and the two are never mixed. Oracle statements are
    labelled.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.  THE FROZEN CHOICE - THE RULE, FIXED BEFORE THE RESULT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The point of this instrument is to STOP tuning the penalty. So the rule
    that turns the result into a frozen choice is written down here, before
    the result exists.

    4.1  WHAT IS FROZEN, IN ORDER OF PRECEDENCE

        FIRST, the PROCEDURE. For any future logistic-regression work in this
        project, the penalty is chosen by Instrument 4's nested inner-CV
        selection over the COMBINED thirteen-point grid, fitted on training
        rows only. This is the primary freeze. It is leakage-safe, it adapts
        to a fold's sample size, and it has a proof battery already attached.

        SECOND, a SCALAR, for any context that needs one fixed number rather
        than a procedure - a quick comparison, a diagnostic, a fit where an
        inner CV is not affordable:

            FROZEN_LAMBDA_SCALAR = median over the 28 per-(rung, fold)
                                   selected lambdas from THIS run,
                                   snapped to the nearest grid point,
                                   exact ties going to the LARGER.

    4.2  THE FREEZE IS CONDITIONAL, AND THE CONDITIONS ARE DECLARED NOW

        VALID    no selection lands on 10000. The optimum is bracketed, and
                 both the procedure and the scalar are frozen.

        BLOCKED  any selection lands on 10000. The optimum is still outside
                 the grid; NO scalar is frozen and the artefact records the
                 block. The grid is not extended a second time - a result
                 that keeps running away from every box we build is itself
                 the finding, and it would mean the penalty is not the right
                 lever.

        WEAK     VALID, but the selected lambdas span more than one decade
                 across the 28 cells. A single scalar is then a poor summary
                 of a genuinely fold-dependent quantity; the scalar is still
                 recorded but marked WEAK, and the PROCEDURE freeze governs.

    4.3  THE ARTEFACT

        outputs/phase3_frozen_regularisation.json, carrying: the status above,
        the scalar, the per-(rung, fold) table, the combined grid, the SHA-256
        of both pre-declarations, and the SHA-256 of every input it was
        derived from. Written once by this instrument. Nothing else in the
        project writes it.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.  WHAT IS FROZEN AND MUST NOT MOVE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Everything Instrument 4 froze, PLUS Instrument 4's own outputs:

        outputs/phase3_reg_*.csv                   Instrument 4's results
        PHASE3_REGULARISATION_PREDECLARATION.txt   its declaration
        outputs/phase3_ablation_*.csv              the lambda = 1 reference
        outputs/phase[012]_*                       Phase 0/1/2
        data/raw/**                                the raw data

    Instrument 5 writes only outputs/phase3_ceiling_*.csv and the frozen
    regularisation JSON. Every file above is SHA-256'd before and after; one
    changed hash is a FAIL.

    The single exception, declared in section 2, is
    scripts/phase3_regularisation_sensitivity.py, which gains the optional
    `grid` argument. Its behaviour is unchanged and C1 proves it.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.  THE TESTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Instrument 4's full battery is re-run against this grid, unchanged and
    imported rather than copied:

    R1   no inner-CV row is an outer-test row
    R2   corrupting outer-test FEATURES moves no selected lambda (+ control)
    R3   shuffling outer-test LABELS moves no selected lambda
    R4   the runtime tripwire, plus a control proving it can fire
    R5   every inner split is strictly forward in time
    R6   the grid used is the grid declared in THIS file
    R8   nothing frozen moved; data/raw was only hashed
    R9   a second full run is bit-identical
    R10  B0's fit is invariant across the grid
    R11  convergence, and the floor/ceiling report

    R7 (reproduce Instrument 3 at lambda = 1) does not apply - lambda = 1 is
    not in this grid. It is replaced by:

    C1   at lambda = 100, this instrument reproduces Instrument 4's stored
         lambda = 100 results to < 1e-9, all six metrics, all 28 rung x fold
         pairs. This is the anchor, and it also proves the section 2 refactor
         changed nothing.

    C2   the freeze rule was applied exactly as declared in section 4: the
         status follows the declared conditions, and the scalar is the
         declared median-snapped-to-grid of the selections actually made.

    C3   the grid now reaches the degenerate end. At lambda = 10000 the
         fitted coefficient norm must be a small fraction of its value at
         lambda = 100, and every rung's predictions must be measurably closer
         to B0's than they are at lambda = 100. Without this, a grid that
         merely failed to find a turning point could not be distinguished
         from one that never got near the asymptote.

END OF PRE-DECLARATION

===============================================================================
AMENDMENT 2 - 2026-08-31
THE FREEZE RULE OPERATES OVER RUNGS THAT HAVE PENALISED PARAMETERS
===============================================================================

WHAT WAS FOUND

    The run applied section 4's rule literally and returned BLOCKED. All four
    ceiling hits were B0, and B0 alone:

        B0   10000  10000  10000  10000
        B1    1000    300    300    300
        B2     3000   1000   1000   1000
        B3     3000   1000   1000   1000
        B4     3000   1000   1000   1000
        B5     3000   1000   1000   1000
        B6     3000   1000   1000   1000

    B0 has ZERO penalised design columns. The intercept is unpenalised, so
    lambda multiplies nothing, so every lambda produces an identical fit -
    which is not an inference, it is test R10, and R10 passed at 0.000e+00 in
    this very run. B0's inner-CV curve is therefore exactly constant, and the
    declared tie-break ("exact ties go to the LARGER lambda") mechanically
    returns the largest grid point.

    So the BLOCKED status was produced by a rung whose selection carries no
    information about where the penalty optimum lies. Section 4.2 intended
    BLOCKED to mean "the optimum is still outside the grid". Here it meant
    "a constant function was maximised by the tie-break".

    Section 4.6 of Instrument 4's pre-declaration already anticipated the
    underlying fact - "B0 carries no features ... every lambda gives an
    identical fit" - and made it an assertion rather than an assumption. What
    was not anticipated was that the freeze rule would then read that
    degenerate selection as a boundary hit.

THE AMENDMENT

    Section 4 is replaced by:

        The freeze rule - the median, the spread test and the ceiling test -
        operates over selections from rungs with AT LEAST ONE PENALISED
        DESIGN COLUMN. A rung with none is reported in every table and
        excluded from the rule.

        The test is on the design matrix, not on the rung's name: a rung
        qualifies if its fitted design has more than zero columns. Nothing
        is hardcoded to "B0".

    Nothing else changes. Not the grid, not the protocol, not the selection
    procedure, not the tie-break, not the three status conditions themselves,
    and not section 3's questions.

    Under the amended rule:

        n = 24, min 300, max 3000, median 1000, spread exactly 1.000 decades,
        ceiling hits 0   ->   STATUS VALID, FROZEN SCALAR lambda = 1000

WHAT HAD BEEN SEEN WHEN THIS AMENDMENT WAS WRITTEN, AND WHO DECIDED IT

    Disclosed in full, because this amendment CHANGES THE OUTCOME - from
    BLOCKED with no scalar, to VALID with lambda = 1000 - and it was written
    after the result was known. That is the opposite of the ordering this
    project's pre-declarations exist to enforce, so it is recorded rather
    than smoothed over.

    SEEN    the complete selection table above
    SEEN    the full outer-test surface and the combined 13-point grid
    SEEN    that every featured rung's optimum is interior at lambda = 1000
    SEEN    the rung verdict (UPHELD) and both C3 failures

    The amendment was NOT applied by the agent on its own judgement. It was
    put to the project owner with the literal BLOCKED outcome, the alternative
    of accepting BLOCKED unchanged, and a third option of freezing per-rung
    instead; the owner chose this amendment.

    THE ARGUMENT THAT IT IS NOT OUTCOME-DRIVEN, for the reader to weigh:

    The exclusion follows from the mathematics of the objective, not from
    this dataset. A rung with no penalised parameters has a constant
    lambda-curve on ANY data, so its selection is always the tie-break's
    output and never evidence about the optimum. The amended rule would have
    been the correct rule before any number was seen.

    And it does not disarm the condition it was written for: had B1-B6
    themselves selected 10000, the status would still be BLOCKED under the
    amended rule, because those rungs do have penalised parameters. The
    amendment removes a false positive; it does not remove the alarm.

    The reader who rejects that argument should read the frozen artefact as
    BLOCKED with no scalar, and use the PROCEDURE freeze alone. Both numbers
    are in the record.

C3 REMAINS FAILED, AND IS NOT AMENDED

    C3a (0.1212 against a threshold of 0.10) and C3b (B6 sits marginally
    further from B0 at lambda = 10000 than at lambda = 100) both failed and
    are left failing.

    They are correct tests of a claim that was pitched too high. C3 asserted
    the grid reaches the DEGENERATE end; resolving the ceiling only requires
    that it BRACKETS an interior optimum, which it does for all six featured
    rungs, with the curve rising on both sides of lambda = 1000.

    The C3a threshold was justified in the code comment by an argument that
    ridge norms decay as 1/lambda, predicting a ratio near 0.01 over two
    decades. That argument does not hold here: at lambda = 10000 the penalty
    is still only comparable to the Fisher information at n = 1520, so the
    fit is at the start of the asymptotic regime, not deep in it. The
    observed decay is 0.4802 to 0.0582, roughly eightfold.

    Lowering the threshold after seeing 0.1212 would be exactly the failure
    mode these documents exist to prevent. The failures stand.
